# Thumbnail — What If You Got 2x Heavier Every Single Day?

Made by the user in SeaArt/Midjourney from this prompt (rules: CLAUDE.md and `docs/13-thumbnail-patterns.md`).

## Prompt v3 — our own look (Oct 20)
Why v1/v2 looked fake: photoreal car, river and lava pasted around a cartoon figure, the car wasn't hurt, the
lava + splash + purple sunset is the generic AI look, and the figure was a tiny head on stilt legs.
The idea in 5 words: **sunk waist-deep into the road.** It reads at phone size and nobody else has it.

```
16:9 aspect ratio, YouTube thumbnail. Cinematic 2.5D 3D render of a slender stick-figure with thin limbs, a solid black spherical head, and glowing white eyes. Wearing a brightly colored hoodie.
STYLE: one consistent stylized 3D world, like a frame from a premium animated short: smooth matte materials, simplified shapes, clean edges, soft global illumination. Everything in the scene, road, car and houses, is stylized and simplified to match the character; nothing photorealistic.
SHOT: low camera close to the ground, wide lens. The stick-figure is sunk up to his waist into a solid asphalt road, as if the road were quicksand; only his royal-blue hoodie, his arms and his big round head stick out. His head fills about 40% of the frame height, on the right third of the frame. Calm, unbothered face: half-closed flat white eyes and a small sideways smirk, not angry. His arms are folded and resting on the road surface.
CONSEQUENCE: around him the asphalt breaks into a perfect circular crater with deep cracks glowing warm gold, slabs of road tilting down toward him, a small red car at the crater edge tipping in nose-first.
BACKGROUND: quiet suburban street at dusk, out of focus, warm streetlights.
SIGNATURE: a thin glowing cyan rim light outlines the stick-figure, like he comes from a parallel world; all other light is warm gold and orange.
COMPOSITION: one subject, clean; the left 40% of the frame is dark smooth sky and blurred street with nothing in it, empty space for big text.
Strictly NO human skin, NO realistic faces, NO thick/chibi 3D bodies, NO Pixar styles. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no signs.
```
- SeaArt negative prompt: `photorealistic, photo, realistic car, lava, fire, water splash, purple sunset, clutter, many objects, text, letters, logo, sign, license plate, angry eyes, evil eyes, long legs, human skin, realistic face, thick body, chibi, Pixar`

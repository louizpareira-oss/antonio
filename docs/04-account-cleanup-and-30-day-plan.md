# 4. Account cleanup and 30-day plan

TikTok judged the **account**, not one video. So the account has to visibly change before
re-applying. A reviewer opening your profile should see a different page.

## Step 1 — Clean the profile (do this first, this week)

Go through your posts and sort them into three piles:

**Delete** — videos built entirely from downloaded GIFs/anime/cartoon clips with an AI
voice over a borrowed quote. These are the evidence for the rejection. As long as they
are on the profile, the rejection reason stays true.

**Keep but set to private** — your best performers you do not want to lose
(the 2.1M and 148.5K videos). Private posts keep their data but are removed from the
public profile a reviewer sees. You lose nothing permanently.

**Keep public** — anything with your own footage, your own voice, or a substantially
original edit. Probably very few right now. That is fine.

Be willing to end up with an almost-empty profile. An empty profile is neutral; a profile
full of flagged-format videos is a documented violation.

## Step 2 — Update the profile itself

- **Bio:** "Love" is not a bio. Say who this page is for and what they get.
  Example: *"Notes on discipline, solitude, and the things nobody says out loud. New video most days."*
- **Profile picture:** the AI knight is fine and on-brand — keep it if the page stays
  faceless. If you go Route C, use your face.
- **Pinned posts:** unpin the old-format videos. Pin the first three new-format ones as
  they go up.

## Step 3 — The 30 days

| Days | What you do |
|---|---|
| **1–3** | Cleanup above. Pick Route A, B, or C. Write 10 scripts from your own life. |
| **4–7** | Build the asset library. Route A: one long filming session, 40+ clips. Route B: generate 40+ vertical clips across your moods. |
| **8–30** | Post **1 video per day, minimum 65 seconds, 1080×1920, full screen.** No exceptions, no skipped days. |
| **31+** | Re-apply. Not before. |

23 days × 1 post = 23 new-format videos on the profile against a clean slate.
That is a page a reviewer can approve.

## Why 30 days and not tomorrow

Re-applying now, with the same profile, gets the same answer, and repeated rejections
make the next appeal harder. The clock is not punishment — it is the time it takes to
build the evidence that the appeal in [`05-appeal-template.md`](05-appeal-template.md)
points at.

## While you wait — the account still earns

Creator Rewards is one income stream and honestly not the biggest one at 12.5K followers.
These do not require approval and you can start this week:

- **Your own digital product.** You now know exactly what a $44 "viral video course"
  contains, and you have a 2.1M-view video as proof of work. You can make a genuinely
  better one — one that does not get people demonetized.
- **TikTok Shop / affiliate** — books fit your niche perfectly. Stoicism, psychology,
  discipline. Commission per sale, no Creator Rewards approval needed.
- **Brand deals** — 12.5K with 535K likes is a real engagement rate. Small brands pay
  $100–300 per post at that size. Put an email in your bio.
- **Live gifts** — separate program, separate approval, much lower bar.
- **Newsletter / Stan store** — the person who sold you the course did exactly this.
  That part of their business model is worth copying. The video format is not.

# Script 02 — "Golden Son"

**Target length:** 70 seconds (10 scenes, 0:00–1:10)
**Format:** 1080 × 1920, full bleed, no bars
**Style:** Stylized 3D animation — Pixar-grade polish, urban street-style character design
**Register:** Deadpan absurdist satire. Every line lands flat. Nobody in the film thinks it is funny.

---

## The idea underneath

Script 01 was sincere. This one is the opposite lane, and the account needs both: a serious
lane and a comedy lane, or the grid reads as one repeated template — which is a named item
on the auto-fail checklist in [`docs/03-technical-spec.md`](../docs/03-technical-spec.md).

The joke is not "one bird is dirty." The joke is that **the father is sorting his sons
before either of them has done anything.** Gold and mud get poured on them in the first
five seconds, and everything after that is the two birds living inside a decision that was
made for them. The turn is that the sorting was wrong — and the father still will not
revise it.

> A parent does not usually pick a favourite by merit. They pick one, and then spend
> eighteen years collecting evidence.

The controlling image is **the pour**: Scene 1 is gold onto one son, mud onto the other.
Scene 10 is the *same framing*, the same room, the same camera — and the pour has swapped
hands. Same frame, changed meaning. This is the same matched-pair technique as the doorway
in Script 01 (beats 3 and 20), and it is the reason people rewatch. **Do not cut Scene 10.**

## Characters

Lock these descriptions. Paste the same wording into every clip prompt so the birds stay
consistent between generations — and lock the seed if your tool has one.

| Character | Description | Voice |
|---|---|---|
| **Pop-cuh** (the leader) | Adult pigeon, slate-grey feathers, **gold motorcycle-style helmet**, heavy gold chains, gold rings on both feet, tracksuit. Immaculately groomed. | Deep, posh, stern, completely humourless |
| **Golden Son** | Young pigeon, gleaming iridescent gold-dipped feathers, small gold helmet, gold chain. Vacant, contented expression. | Smug, slow, nasal |
| **Poopy Son** (the dirty one) | Young pigeon, matted brown feathers clumped and distressed, one bent tail feather, a plastic bottle-cap worn as a helmet. Bright, alert eyes — he is the awake one. | Hopeful, squeaky, earnest |
| **Narrator** | Not seen. | Deep, gravelly, cynical |

**The eyes rule:** Poopy Son's eyes are always the sharpest thing in the frame. The audience
should clock, before the script tells them, that the wrong bird got the gold.

## Style bible

Paste the relevant block into every prompt. Consistency across clips is what makes a set of
AI generations read as one film instead of a folder of stock clips.

**Visual texture:** Highly stylized 3D character animation, Pixar-esque polish, edgy urban
street-style character design. Tactile feather grooming — soft and preened on the gold
birds, matted, gritty and distressed on the dirty bird.

**Lighting:** Cinematic high-end production. Soft natural sunlight through floor-to-ceiling
windows, warm golden-hour rim light picking out the metallic reflections on gold helmets
and jewellery.

**Palette:** Hard split. Warm luxurious gold and amber in the elite spaces; drab,
desaturated, muddy brown in the poopy ones. Never let the two palettes blend except in
Scene 10, where they finally swap.

**Camera:** Mostly static medium-to-wide. Balanced, symmetrical, framed like a high-end
corporate or lifestyle commercial. 35–50mm. Deep depth of field on the wides, narrowing for
expression close-ups.

**Mood:** Absurdist, rhythmic, deadpan.

## Scene breakdown

Scenes 1–3 are the brief as written. Scenes 4–10 carry it to a monetizable length — the
Creator Rewards threshold is 60 seconds, so a 15-second cut of this **cannot earn**, no
matter how well it performs. See "If you only want the 15" below before you shorten it.

### Scene 1 — The pour (0:00–0:05)

Symmetrical medium-long shot. Bright modern office/living space, floor-to-ceiling windows.
Pop-cuh stands dead centre. He pours **molten gold** onto the posh pigeon on his left and
thick **brown mud-like liquid** onto the bedraggled pigeon on his right. Clean,
architectural, sharply contrasted.

- Twitchy, realistic pigeon head movements; blinking.
- Both liquids flow with heavy, viscous physics.
- Dust motes catch the window light in the background.

> **NARRATOR** *(deep, gravelly, cynical)*: "Some birds are born into golden nests. Others are destined for the gutter."

**Sound:** Slow lo-fi hip-hop beat, heavy bassline. Under it: the squish of mud and the
bright metallic ring of pouring gold.

### Scene 2 — The patio (0:05–0:09)

Wide shot, sun-drenched patio outside a modern mansion. Pop-cuh embraces Golden Son. Poopy
Son stands off to the side, dejected. Warm, inviting light — the environment is doing the
bragging.

- Pop-cuh pats Golden Son on the back.
- Poopy Son shifts his weight, visibly out of place.
- Camera pans subtly to hold the central interaction.

> **POP-CUH** *(stern, posh)*: "I love you, my golden son, cuh."
> **POOPY SON** *(hopeful, squeaky)*: "What about me, pop-cuh?"
> **POP-CUH** *(cold)*: "Shut up, cuh."

**Sound:** Beat continues. Distant lawnmower, faint birdsong.

### Scene 3 — The car (0:09–0:15)

Interior car shot, all three birds. Shifting light across their faces as the car moves.
Tight framing — the social hierarchy is the composition.

- Pop-cuh's foot on the wheel, heavy gold ring.
- The sons look out of windows, then at each other.
- Trees and power lines slide across the window reflections.

> **POP-CUH**: "First we drop your brother at Poopy Poop High. Then we drop you at Golden High."
> **POOPY SON**: "Pop-cuh, I want to go to Golden High."
> **POP-CUH**: "Hell nah, cuh."

**Sound:** Muffled engine hum, tyres on pavement, beat continuing.

### Scene 4 — Poopy Poop High (0:15–0:22)

Exterior wide. A sagging brown school building, cracked sign, standing puddles, a lone
pigeon asleep on the steps. The gleaming car does not stop — it slows. Poopy Son is
deposited into a puddle. The car is already moving off.

- The door opens and shuts in one motion; no one looks back.
- Poopy Son lands, straightens his bottle-cap helmet, keeps his chin up.
- Mud ripples outward in slow, heavy physics — rhyming with Scene 1's pour.

> **POOPY SON**: "Pop-cuh, the car's still moving."
> **POP-CUH** *(not turning around)*: "Then roll, cuh."

**Sound:** Tyres, a wet splash, one sad pigeon coo in the distance.

### Scene 5 — Golden High (0:22–0:29)

Exterior wide, and the exact opposite: white marble steps, gold banners, topiary, a valet
pigeon in livery. Symmetrical, pristine, absurdly over-lit. The car stops properly here.

- Pop-cuh opens the door for Golden Son personally.
- Golden Son walks up the steps in slow motion for no reason at all.
- Lens flare off every gold surface. Play it completely straight.

> **POP-CUH** *(warm for the only time in the film)*: "Have a golden day, my golden son."
> **GOLDEN SON** *(smug)*: "Aight, pop-cuh."

**Sound:** Beat swells. A distant string section, a touch too grand.

### Scene 6 — Poopy Poop High, interior (0:29–0:37)

Classroom. Cracked chalkboard, leaking ceiling, three students. Poopy Son is at the board,
and the board is covered in genuinely immaculate mathematics. The teacher stares. Hold the
shot one beat too long.

- Slow push in on the chalkboard, then to his eyes — the sharpest thing in frame.
- Water drips into a bucket, in time with the beat.

> **TEACHER** *(flat, exhausted)*: "That's... correct, cuh."
> **POOPY SON** *(quiet)*: "I know, cuh."

**Sound:** Beat drops to just the bassline. Drip. Chalk.

### Scene 7 — Golden High, interior (0:37–0:44)

A lecture theatre of polished gold. Golden Son sits front row, eating a gold crayon. On the
board behind him: **2 + 2 = ?**. He raises a wing with total confidence.

- Static symmetrical wide, then a slow push to his vacant, contented face.
- He chews. The crayon is visibly a crayon.

> **GOLDEN SON** *(certain)*: "Gold."
> **PROFESSOR** *(after a long pause)*: "...Outstanding."

**Sound:** Beat, and one single triumphant horn stab that does not belong.

### Scene 8 — The dinner table (0:44–0:53)

Formal dining room, absurdly long table. Pop-cuh at the head, a son at each far end. Two
report cards slide down the table. Symmetrical, golden-hour light through tall windows.

- Pop-cuh reads the first card. Reads the second. Reads the first again.
- Poopy Son's straight A's. Golden Son's straight F's.
- Full 2-second silent hold on Pop-cuh's face. The film stops breathing.

> **POP-CUH** *(to Poopy Son, dead flat)*: "Who did you copy, cuh?"
> **POOPY SON**: "Nobody, pop-cuh."
> **POP-CUH**: "Hell nah, cuh."

**Sound:** Beat stops entirely on the silent hold. A clock ticks. The beat returns on
"Hell nah."

### Scene 9 — Graduation (0:53–1:02)

Years later. Split composition, one frame: on the left, Poopy Son in a mud-brown cap and
gown holding a real diploma, feathers still matted, standing straight. On the right, Golden
Son in a gold tracksuit holding a cardboard sign reading **"WILL POSE FOR FOOD."**

- Both look directly down the lens. Neither one blinks for three full seconds.
- The light has flattened — for the first time the two palettes sit in the same frame.

> **NARRATOR**: "The gold never asked to be poured. Neither did the mud."

**Sound:** The beat, stripped back to a single repeated piano note.

### Scene 10 — The pour, reversed (1:02–1:10)

**THE MATCHED PAIR.** Identical framing to Scene 1: same room, same windows, same camera
height, same symmetry. Poopy Son now stands dead centre, groomed and in a plain grey suit —
no gold anywhere. He pours **molten gold** onto Pop-cuh on his left, and **mud** onto Golden
Son on his right.

- Same viscous physics, same dust motes, same twitchy blinks.
- Poopy Son does not smile. He does not gloat. He just pours.
- Final beat: he sets the buckets down and walks out of frame. Hold the empty centre for
  two full seconds under the last line.

> **POP-CUH** *(quiet, gold running off his helmet)*: "...I love you, cuh."
> **POOPY SON** *(not turning around)*: "I know, cuh."

**Sound:** Beat resolves on the downbeat as he exits. Then silence, then one mud drip.

## Voice direction

Four voices. If you are recording all of them yourself, record each character in a separate
pass, all the way through — do not switch voices line by line, you will lose the pitch.

- **Narrator:** slow, gravelly, bored of being right. Two lines only, Scenes 1 and 9.
- **Pop-cuh:** posh and clipped, never raises his voice. "Hell nah, cuh" is said at exactly
  the same pitch and speed both times — that repetition is the joke.
- **Golden Son:** slow, nasal, unbothered. Four words in the whole film.
- **Poopy Son:** squeaky and hopeful for the first half; in Scenes 6 and 10 he drops
  **half an octave** and gets quiet. That drop is the entire character arc. Do not overplay
  it — it should be barely noticeable on the first watch and obvious on the second.

**The tic:** "cuh" ends most lines. It is a rhythm instrument, not a joke — say it flat,
never lean on it. The one line without it (Scene 5, "Have a golden day, my golden son") is
the only warmth Pop-cuh shows anyone, and it stands out because the tic is missing.

## Captions

2–4 words at a time, changing with the voice. Centred, inside the safe zone (12% clear top,
20% bottom, 20% right — see [`docs/03-technical-spec.md`](../docs/03-technical-spec.md)).

Break them like this:

```
Some birds / are born / into golden nests
I love you / my golden son / cuh
What about me / pop-cuh?
Shut up, cuh.
Who did you copy / cuh?
I love you / cuh.
I know / cuh.
```

Give the last two cards extra time. That exchange is the screenshot.

**Do not caption the narrator's lines in the same style as dialogue** — use a different
position (lower third, smaller) so the viewer can tell who is speaking without hearing it.
Most of your views are muted for the first second.

## Alternative hooks (test these)

The first three seconds decide it. Keep the whole film and swap only the opening:

1. Scene 1 as written — the pour, narrator over it. *(default)*
2. Cold open on **"Shut up, cuh"** — start on Scene 2's punchline, then cut back to the
   pour. Fastest possible entry into the joke.
3. Cold open on Scene 8's silent hold — Pop-cuh reading the report cards — then jump back
   to the pour. Starts on the mystery.
4. Narrator first, over black, one second: *"He picked a favourite before either of them
   could fly."* Then the pour.

Hook 2 is the most aggressive and will probably test best: the punchline arrives before the
viewer's thumb has decided anything.

## If you only want the 15

The three scenes in the original brief are a complete comic unit and will work as a short.
But **a 15-second video earns nothing** — Creator Rewards pays only over 60 seconds, which
is the entire reason this repo exists. Two honest options:

- **Post the 70.** Scenes 4–10 are written to the same rhythm; the turn in Scene 6 is what
  makes it rewatchable rather than just a gag.
- **Post the 15 as a trailer** for the 70, on the same day, and accept that the short one
  is promotion, not income. Do not post the short one alone and call it a monetized upload.

What you must not do is pad to 61 seconds with holds. Reviewers read that instantly, and
"low quality content" is the exact rejection you are already appealing.

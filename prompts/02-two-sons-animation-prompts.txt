This file is only for animating the 16 approved stills from

HOW TO USE
- For each clip, upload the matching still as the FIRST FRAME,
  then paste the prompt below as the motion description.
- Clip lengths are in each prompt. Most are 5 seconds; clips 7,
  11 and 16 are 3.
- If a take is bad, re-run it on the SAME still. Never regenerate
  the still - that is how the characters drift.
- The characters are pigeon heads on human bodies. Watch every
  take for a head turning human, or an arm turning into a wing
  halfway through. That is a re-run, not a keeper.
- Generate 3 takes of each, keep the best one.
- Turn ON TikTok's AI-generated content label when you upload.

THE RULES THESE PROMPTS FOLLOW (keep them if you write your own)
- One action per clip. Name it, then name what must NOT move.
- Say "camera completely locked" on every static shot. Video
  models add drift and push-in unless you forbid it.
- Ask for small motion. Big motion is where faces melt.
- Liquids: always say heavy, thick, slow, viscous. Default
  physics look like water and kill the joke.
- Blinks and one small head move read as "alive" without risking
  the face. Use them instead of performance.

NEGATIVE PROMPT (paste into the negative field for every clip)
morphing, warping face, head turning human, wings replacing arms,
feathered arms, changing costume, character swap, extra characters
appearing, camera shake, handheld, fast motion, zoom, text,
watermark, subtitles

## Sets and palettes
- Dusk/twilight dominates (1, 4, 5): purple-to-peach skies, dark facades with warm windows, lamp cones with dust.
- Ifverse interiors (2, 3): olive-sage kitchen + beige walls, slate bedroom with desk lamp vs moonlight, dark call centre with cyan/green monitors, black "tactical map" spaces with gold circuit lines.
- One Small If (6) is bright daylight: cream walls, sage cabinets, beige checker tiles, magenta rug.
- Diagrams: navy blueprint grid with white/cyan/orange line art (4, 5), or black with gold traces (3).

## Camera, graphics, editing (shared)
- Shots every 1.5–3 s; wide → medium → giant face → insert → top-down rotation; freeze/slow-motion contrast.
- Letterbox bars: constant in 2, 3, 6; snap in for action in 5; none in 4.
- Numbers on screen in gold/cream bold sans; dark rounded "HUD pill" badges (2, 3, 5, 6); red stamp tags `[FROZEN]` (3); green checkmarks; persistent top-left clock/size HUD (6).
- Torn-paper split screens (2, 4); glitch face mask (3); brain diagram inside the head (1).
- Hook in the first 10–15 s (a strange everyday moment or flash-forward), then the title question.
- Ending: cast line-up + "What would YOU do?" + subscribe pill.
- Voice: calm deep male (2, 3, 6) or upbeat male (1, 4, 5); synth music; whooshes, UI blips, ticks.

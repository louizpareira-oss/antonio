# Idea bank

Batch 1 lives here. Ideas are the cheapest stage and the only one with real leverage — a
weak concept killed on this page costs one paragraph; the same concept killed after
generation costs a day and a pile of clips.

Nothing leaves this file until it has a **claim someone could disagree with**. That is the
gate, and most ideas die at it.

**Status:** `raw` (a hook and nothing else) · `shaped` (has a claim and a payoff) ·
`written` (a script exists) · `parked` (good, wrong moment) · `dead` (kept anyway — dead
ideas cross-pollinate)

---

## The world

These share one premise: **a city where some people have the head of an animal, and
nobody finds it remarkable.** It is never explained, never joked about, and no character
ever mentions it directly. The head is the fact; the story is always about how people
behave around it.

Why it holds together as a body of work:

- **One master character block per story**, but one **shared world block** — same grey
  city, same cel-shaded register, same light. See
  [`docs/03-character-consistency.md`](../docs/03-character-consistency.md).
- The animal supplies the **controlling image** for free. Feeding, moulting, nocturnal,
  swarm. You get doc 01's step 3 handed to you.
- Viewers recognise the format across videos without you making it a gimmick.

**The trap:** the head must never be the punchline. The moment a video is *about* having a
pigeon head, it becomes a sketch and stops landing. It is a fact the character carries, the
way a real person carries a face.

## Shaped

### 01 — The Feeding · `written`
**Claim:** People are not cruel to you. They are comfortable — they met a version of you
before you arrived. The trap is that you will audition for it, alone, where nobody can see.
**Character:** Pigeon head. Accounts payable. Navy overcoat, lanyard.
**Image:** Feeding — bread, crumbs, flocks, buying your own bag.
**Hook:** *"The first one hit me in the chest."*
**Payoff:** An old woman uses his name. Nobody had in a year.
→ [`scripts/01-the-feeding.md`](../scripts/01-the-feeding.md)

### 09 — No Rush · `written`
**Claim:** Nobody has ever been unkind to me. They have been patient — and patience is how
people tell you that you cost them something, without ever having to say it. The kindness
is real, which is what makes it unanswerable.
**Character:** Tortoise head. Forty-one. Archives. Corduroy jacket, reading glasses.
**Image:** The meter — patience as something spent, watched and itemised. Held doors,
kettles, running numbers, receipts, counted stairs.
**Hook:** *"He's been holding that door for nine seconds."*
**Payoff:** A colleague falls into step at his pace and says *"Same time tomorrow?"* — not
waiting for him, arriving with him.
→ [`scripts/02-no-rush.md`](../scripts/02-no-rush.md)

### 02 — Good Boy · `shaped`
**Claim:** Being liked is not being known. The version of you people love is the one that
costs the most to maintain, and you will keep paying because the applause is real.
**Character:** Dog head. Genuinely beloved. Promoted for an enthusiasm he stopped feeling
years ago.
**Image:** Praise as feeding — being told he is good, constantly, by people who have never
asked him anything.
**Hook:** *"They clapped when I came in. They clap every morning."*
**Payoff:** He says one true thing in a meeting. The room goes quiet, then someone laughs
and calls him a character, and the clapping resumes.
**Counterpoint to 01:** same mechanism, opposite temperature. Being adored can isolate you
exactly as efficiently as being fed.

### 03 — Vermin · `shaped`
**Claim:** Which version of unwanted you are is arbitrary. The same city that feeds one of
you crosses the road from another, and neither of you did anything.
**Character:** Rat head. Same city as 01. Night bus, late shifts, a good coat he keeps
clean.
**Image:** Distance — the gap people leave, seats that stay empty, a carriage that thins out.
**Hook:** *"There's a seat free next to me. There's always a seat free next to me."*
**Payoff:** He sees a pigeon-headed man on a bench being handed bread by a stranger, and
understands there is no rule, only luck.
**Note:** the strongest possible companion piece to 01. Shoot second, never first — it
only lands once the audience knows the world.

### 04 — Molt · `shaped`
**Claim:** People congratulate you for changing and never ask what it cost. The change they
are applauding is the part of you that you buried.
**Character:** Pigeon or crow head, mid-forties, feathers thinning. Recently "doing so well."
**Image:** Moulting — feathers in a sink, on a pillow, brushed off a collar in a lift.
**Hook:** *"Everyone keeps telling me I look well."*
**Payoff:** He finds one feather in his daughter's hair from where she hugged him, and
keeps it.

### 05 — Night Shift · `shaped`
**Claim:** You did not choose to be different. You chose a schedule, once, for money — and
then the schedule chose your friends, your food, your decade.
**Character:** Owl head. Awake at 4am. Not lonely in the way people assume.
**Image:** Light — the wrong light at the wrong hour. Supermarket strip-light at 5am, sun
through a closed curtain.
**Hook:** *"I have not seen my best friend in daylight since 2019."*
**Payoff:** He is invited to a wedding at 2pm. He goes. He is the only one squinting, and
he stays anyway.

### 06 — The Hive · `shaped`
**Claim:** Belonging and being used feel identical from the inside. That is not a mistake
you made — it is the design.
**Character:** Bee head. Loves the team. Genuinely, not ironically.
**Image:** The swarm — synchronised movement, identical desks, a shared rhythm that is
beautiful right up until it is not.
**Hook:** *"I was employee of the month four times. I want to tell you what that cost."*
**Payoff:** The team is restructured. It re-forms without him and moves exactly as well.

## Working rules

1. **Ten hooks per sitting**, using the generator in section 1 of
   [`prompts/00-master-prompt-library.txt`](../prompts/00-master-prompt-library.txt).
2. **Keep three.** Score against the four questions there — arguable, first shot nameable,
   opens inside the situation, payoff can deliver.
3. **File the other seven here as `raw`.** They cross-pollinate. Idea 03 came from a
   discarded hook about empty seats.
4. **Promote to `shaped`** only when it has a claim, a controlling image, a hook line and a
   payoff direction — the four things [`docs/01-start-with-the-story.md`](../docs/01-start-with-the-story.md)
   needs before writing.
5. **One `written` at a time.** A second script started before the first is shot is a
   second script that never gets shot.

Why Myth POV: "POV" is the Shorts word for second-person stories, so the name says the format; "Myth" says the topic;
short, easy to say, works as a watermark and as a search term. If the handle is taken, try @MythPOVShorts or @TheMythPOV.

## About (YouTube channel description)
> You wake up inside a myth. You're the god, the hero, the monster, or the fool who made a deal with one.
> Every Short is a real story from Norse, Greek, Egyptian, Chinese, Japanese and other mythologies, told to YOU,
> animated in 3D, in about a minute. New myth every day.

(Short version for TikTok/Instagram bio, max 80 chars: `Real myths. You're the hero. One minute each. 🔱`)

## Links / contact
- Business email: create a new one for this channel (e.g. mythpov.business@gmail.com). Do not reuse older channels' emails.
- No links in Shorts descriptions.

## Voice
- Narrator: one designed ElevenLabs voice, male or female, warm, slightly amused, conversational, fast (200–230 wpm),
  clear consonants, no "movie trailer" boom. Design prompt: "Warm, clever storyteller in his 30s, American, relaxed but
  fast, slight smile in the voice, talks to the listener like a friend telling a crazy story, clean studio sound."
- Quoted characters (Loki, a god, a giantess): 1–2 extra designed voices, used for one line each at most.

## Profile picture prompt (SeaArt / any image tool, the user makes it)

light across the chest, wearing a dark hooded cloak, centred head-and-shoulders, deep teal background with a faint golden
rune circle, high contrast, readable at 48 px, no text."

## Defaults
- Format: YouTube Shorts 9:16 1080×1920 30 fps, 60–85 s. Same file on TikTok and Instagram Reels.
- Posting: 1 per day, same hour (test 2 slots for 2 weeks, keep the better one).
- Language: English, simple words (grade 6–8), present tense, "you".

# Script formula

## Rules
- 230–300 words, 65–85 s at 200–215 wpm. Present tense. "You" from the first word to the last.
- Short clauses (3–9 words) that cut into 1–4-word captions. One idea per sentence.
- Every concrete noun or number in a sentence = one picture (plan it in the shot list).
- Names once, then "you", "he", "she". Max 4 named characters.
- One quoted line per side character, max 2 in the video.
- Facts must be true to the myth's main sources (Prose Edda, Poetic Edda, Hesiod, Homer, Ovid, Book of the Dead,
  Journey to the West, Kojiki…). Write the source in `story/sources.md`.

## Beat sheet (seconds for a 75 s video)
| Beat | Time | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–3 | The impossible situation, in the middle: "You can't be hurt. So the gods throw axes at you. For fun." |
| Who you are | 3–7 | "You're <name>, <role in 4 words>." |
| The rule / deal | 7–22 | How the power or the deal works + 3 quick examples (a list montage). |
| The game | 22–38 | You use it; it escalates; the world reacts. |
| The catch | 38–55 | The trickster/flaw: "But one thing never promised." |
| The cost | 55–68 | Loss or punishment; a pause; the grade punctuation. |
| Twist + loop | 68–75 | One last true detail that flips the story or points back to the first line. End on a question or a callback, never "subscribe". |

## Hook patterns (fill with our own words)
- "Imagine you <did the impossible>. Now <consequence>."
- "You <have the power>. That's the problem."
- "You made one bet with <god>. You lost <something huge>."
- "Everyone in <world> <does X> to you. You're the only one who <Y>."

## 12 pilot topics (own wording, no reference overlaps)
1. Norse — You're Tyr: you put your hand in the wolf's mouth so the gods can lie to him.
2. Norse — You're Thor in a dress: your hammer was stolen, and the giant wants a bride.
3. Greek — You're Sisyphus: you chained Death, so nobody on Earth can die.
4. Greek — You're Arachne: you beat a goddess at weaving, and that's your mistake.
5. Greek — You're Icarus's father: you built the wings, and you told him the rule.
6. Egyptian — You're dead, and your heart is about to be weighed against a feather.
7. Egyptian — You're Ra's daughter: he sends you to punish humans, and you can't stop.
8. Chinese — You're Hou Yi: there are ten suns in the sky, and you have ten arrows.
9. Japanese — You're Izanagi: you went into the underworld for your wife, and you looked.
10. Japanese — You're Susanoo: you get eight bottles of sake ready for an eight-headed snake.
11. Mesopotamian — You're Gilgamesh: you found the plant that makes you live forever. Then you took a bath.
12. Celtic — You're Cú Chulainn: you swore never to refuse food, and never to eat dog.

## Script file layout (`scripts/myth/NN-<slug>.md`)
Title options (3) · Hook line · Full narration (one sentence per line) · Quoted lines with voice · Sources · Twist note.

# 5. Re-application template

## Why the last appeal did not work

This is what you submitted on Sep 4:

> "Dear TikTok Review Team, I believe this low-quality content flag was a mistake. I put
> significant effort into editing my videos to engage my audience properly. Every video is
> designed to provide entertaining and authentic content rather than spammy or unoriginal
> formats. Please re-evaluate my account for the Creator Rewards Program."

Three problems:

1. **It disputes the finding instead of answering it.** The flag was not a mistake — the
   videos genuinely used downloaded GIFs in a letterboxed 4:3 frame. A reviewer opens the
   profile, sees exactly that, and closes the appeal.
2. **"Significant effort in editing" is not the criterion.** Effort is not originality.
   You can spend six hours editing footage that is not yours, and it stays not yours.
3. **No evidence.** No video IDs, no specific changes, nothing checkable.

## The version to submit — after 30 days of new content, not before

Fill in the brackets with real numbers. Keep it factual. No pleading.

---

> Dear TikTok Review Team,
>
> I am re-applying for the Creator Rewards Program after making substantial changes to my
> account following the previous decision.
>
> I understood the original decision. My earlier videos used third-party clips sourced
> from GIF websites, placed in a 4:3 frame inside a 9:16 canvas, which left large unused
> areas of the screen. I have removed that content from my profile.
>
> Since [DATE], every video I have published is produced differently:
>
> - All visuals are [footage I film myself on my own phone / AI visuals I generate and
>   label using TikTok's AI-generated content tool]. I no longer use downloaded clips
>   from third-party websites.
> - All narration is my own recorded voice.
> - All scripts are written by me from my own experience. I no longer read quotes sourced
>   from other accounts.
> - All videos are exported at 1080×1920, full screen, with no black bars or framing.
> - All videos are longer than 60 seconds.
>
> I have published [NUMBER] videos in this format since [DATE]. Recent examples:
> [VIDEO LINK 1], [VIDEO LINK 2], [VIDEO LINK 3].
>
> I would appreciate a review of the account in its current state.
>
> Thank you,
> Louiz — @legendary_louiz

---

## Rules for submitting

- **Wait the full 30 days.** Submitting early wastes the attempt.
- **Link your three strongest new videos.** Make a reviewer's job take 20 seconds.
- Do not mention the previous appeal, do not argue with the previous decision, do not
  say "I believe this is a mistake."
- If it is rejected again, keep posting the new format and re-apply in another 30 days.
  A second rejection on a genuinely changed profile usually means not enough new videos
  yet, not that it is impossible.

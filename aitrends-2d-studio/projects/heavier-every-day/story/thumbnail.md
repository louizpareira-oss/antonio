# Thumbnail — What If You Got 2x Heavier Every Single Day?

Three original concepts for the user to generate in SeaArt (rules in `CLAUDE.md`). Each prompt starts with the
studio's required style block, word for word. The user adds the 2-word text.

**SeaArt negative prompt (all three):**
`human skin, realistic face, thick body, chibi, Pixar, text, letters, numbers, logo, watermark, signs, clutter, small objects, extra limbs`

---

```
16:9 aspect ratio, YouTube thumbnail. Cinematic 2.5D 3D render of a slender stick-figure with thin limbs, a solid black spherical head, and glowing white eyes, wearing a bright blue hoodie. High contrast, dramatic cyan and neon orange rim lighting, epic disaster scale, 8k octane render. Strictly NO human skin, NO realistic faces, NO thick/chibi bodies.
A massive steel truss bridge over a wide river snaps in half at the exact spot where his single sneaker touches the deck: the deck folds down into a deep V, giant steel beams twist and shear apart, the concrete shatters into glowing orange chunks, and a huge wall of river water erupts upward. He stands calmly at the bottom of the V on the right side of the frame, one foot forward, hands in his hoodie pocket, big round head with calm half-closed eyes, filling about 60% of the frame height. Far across the river, one tiny house with a warm porch light. Stormy dusk sky, low camera, wide angle, deep depth of field blur in the background, one clear subject. The left third of the frame is clean dark sky, empty space for text. No text, no letters, no numbers, no signs.
```
**Text:** **ONE** (white) / **STEP.** (yellow)

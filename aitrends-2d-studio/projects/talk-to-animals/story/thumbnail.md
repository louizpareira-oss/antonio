# Thumbnail — What If You Could Talk to Animals?

Three original concepts for the user to generate in SeaArt (rules in `CLAUDE.md`, Oct 20: 2D cel-shaded, close-up
only, character 50–60 % of the frame). Each prompt starts with the studio's required block, word for word. The user
adds the 2-word text.

**v2 (Oct 22)**, rebuilt with the lessons from the user's second reference set (`docs/13-thumbnail-patterns.md`):
each one shows the power itself in one glance (an animal talking *to him*), a dark or muted scene with a glow behind
his head, text in his own voice, and one optional big overlay extra (a red badge or a red arrow).

**Pick:** upload all three with YouTube's **Test & compare** (up to 3 thumbnails per video) and let it choose.
If only one: **#1 HE TALKS.**, because a stranger can guess the title from it in one second.

**SeaArt negative prompt (all three):**
`3d render, octane, photorealistic, realistic texture, wide shot, tiny character, full street, cross-section, human skin, realistic face, thick body, chibi, Pixar, text, letters, numbers, logo, watermark, signs, clutter, small objects, extra limbs`

---

## 1. "HE TALKS." — his old dog whispers, and the bubble is a strip of bacon
```
16:9 aspect ratio, YouTube thumbnail. 2D cel-shaded cartoon illustration style with bold black outlines, flat vibrant colors, and dramatic comic-book glow. A slender stick-figure with thin limbs, a solid black spherical head, and glowing white eyes, wearing a bright blue hoodie. Close-up: the stick-figure is huge in the foreground, taking up 50-60% of the frame, his facial expression big and readable on a phone screen. High contrast, dramatic cyan and neon orange glow. Strictly NO human skin, NO realistic faces, NO thick/chibi bodies, NO 3D render, NO realistic textures.
Close-up on the right side of the frame: an old golden Labrador with a white muzzle and a red collar leans in and whispers into his ear, and a big glowing white speech bubble pops out of the dog's mouth with nothing inside it except one crispy strip of bacon. He gives the camera a sly half-closed side-eye and a small smug grin, not angry, pointing one finger straight at the lens, the hand big and foreshortened. Bold comic-book pop lines burst around the bubble. A warm orange glow right behind his head so its round outline stands out. Simple flat dark blue kitchen background. The left 40% of the frame is clean flat background, empty space for text. No text, no letters, no numbers, no signs.
```
**Text:** **HE** (white) / **TALKS.** (yellow), left side.
**Optional extra:** a red arrow from the text to the bacon bubble.

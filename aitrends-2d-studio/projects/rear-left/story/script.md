# Rear Left — narration script

Word for word. Plain lines are spoken by the narrator. Lines starting with ▸ are directions and are not spoken.


## Cold open

You pull away from pump six.
A light comes on.
A little red door on your dashboard.

Ten minutes later, it's still on.
Rear left.
The door behind you.
Nobody has opened it in weeks.

You'll want to pull over and slam it shut.
Everyone does.

Rule one.
Don't pull over.

A loose door flickers on bumps.
This one doesn't flicker.
It stays on.
Steady.
At sixty.

Something is holding that door.

Press Lock All.
Once.

Four clicks.
Every door locked.
Now drive.

## Pump six

Back at pump six, you went inside to pay.

You left the car unlocked.
It was only two minutes.

Your bag went on the passenger seat.
Your coffee went in the holder.

Your mirror was tilted down.
You fixed it without thinking.

▸ *SC14 (silent): Plant: the rear-left door is visibly unlatched as the car rolls out.*


## Rule 2

Rule two.
Keep the needle above twenty.

Lock All can't hold a door that isn't latched.
Rear left isn't.

At speed, a hand outside can only hold on.
It needs you slow to pull it open.

Exit twelve.
You have to take it.

You'll want to brake for the ramp.
Everyone brakes for the ramp.
Lift off the gas instead.
Let the curve take the speed.

Forty.
Thirty.
Twenty-two.
The light flickers.
It's pulling harder.

Twenty-one.
The ramp straightens.
Thirty again.

Steady red.


## Rule 3

Rule three.
Don't turn on the dome light.

Your thumb knows where the switch is.

Light the cabin, glance back, done.
Everyone does.
Don't.

A lit cabin shows it which seat you're in.
Even reaching is enough.
Look.
The light just moved to your door.

Hand back on the wheel.

It goes back to rear left.

▸ *SC29 (silent): Plant: a streetlight sweep shows the rear-left cushion dented.*


## Rule 4

Rule four.
At a red light, never shift into park.

The first light on Harlan turns red.
The road is empty.
You stop anyway.

Long lights make people shift into park.

Your car unlocks every door in park.

Foot on the brake.
Stay in drive.

Zero miles an hour.
A chime.
A new light.
Rear seat, left side.
Seatbelt unbuckled.

Rear sensors misread bags.
You know that.
Let it chime.

Green.
Go.
Above twenty.


## Rule 5 (midpoint)

Rule five.
Don't touch the mirror.

It's tilted again.
Up, at the roof.
The bumps did that.

You'll want to set it right.
Everyone does.

See a face at the rear window, and you'll brake.
And braking is slow.

The rear window fogs in one small patch.
That's the cold.
Keep driving.


## Rule 6

Rule six.
Passenger airbag light on?
Both hands on the wheel.

The rear seat light goes out.

Then the small light under your mirror changes.
Passenger airbag.
Off.
Then on.

That sensor wakes for an adult's weight.
Your bag weighs two pounds.
It tips into the footwell.
The seat beside you sinks.

The console lid is still shut.
Your coffee hasn't spilled.

Nothing climbed over.

You'll want to reach right.
Push the bag back.
Feel the seat.
Don't.
Both hands on the wheel.

The rear-left light goes out.
Behind you, the door clicks shut.

It's on your side of the car now.
Leaning on the passenger door.
That's all the sensor feels.


## Rule 7

Rule seven.
Close the garage before you open your door.

Birch Lane.
Forty-one.

You'll want to stop in the driveway.
Run for the front door.
Everyone does.
Don't.
Out there, every door is a way in.

Pull into the garage.

Press the remote on your visor.

Watch the door come down in your side mirror.

The streetlight shrinks to a line under it.
Then nothing.
Sealed.

Now you can shift into park.
Every door unlocks.

Engine off.


## Ending

Here's what the dashboard never told you.

The door light only wakes above five miles an hour.

The door was open before you started the car.
It opened while you paid.

You pressed Lock All.
You locked it in with you.

Every rule kept the car moving.
Every rule kept it sealed.
All the way home.
Into a closed garage.

One instruction could have saved you.
You were told to disregard it.

The dome light comes on by itself.
Now you can see the seat beside you.

The belt pulls out.
Slowly.
Across nothing.
Click.

It isn't going anywhere.

Tomorrow, your dashboard will be clear.
Every door shut.
Passenger airbag, on.

# Short 1: NIGHT SUPPORT 001, the hook

**File:** `out/short1.mp4` (1080×1920, 35 s). Rebuild with `node render.mjs --project projects/ns001-short1`, then
`projects/ns001-short1/make_short.sh`. The Short is the first 35.2 s of the long video, ending on "The seventh is about
who drove.", which leaves a cliffhanger that only the full video answers.

## Upload (after the long video is live)

1. YouTube Studio → Create → Upload → `short1.mp4`. Vertical and under 3 minutes, so YouTube makes it a Short.
2. **Title:** `If Your Car Ever Beeps For The Empty Seat... DON'T LOOK`
3. **Description:**
   ```
   There are 7 rules. Six get you home. The seventh is about who drove.
   Full call: NIGHT SUPPORT // CALL 001 (tap the related video below).
   #analoghorror #horrorstories #cars
   ```
4. **Related video:** pick the long video. This puts a button on the Short that opens the full video, and it's the
   whole point of posting Shorts.
5. "Altered or synthetic content": **Yes** (AI narrator voice), same as the long video.

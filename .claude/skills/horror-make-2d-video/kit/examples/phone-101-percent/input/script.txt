If Your Phone Ever Charges To 101%... DO NOT UNPLUG IT

You walk into your kitchen at three in the morning.
Your phone is on the counter. Charging.
But the battery... reads one hundred and one percent.

If this ever happens to you... there is only one rule.

DO NOT UNPLUG IT.

Subject A unplugged theirs at oh four hundred hours.
The screaming started twelve seconds later and hasn't stopped.

Step one. Remain calm.
The thing in the phone feeds on your reaction.
If you panic, it wins.

Step two. Do not make eye contact with the screen.
Even when it's off.
Especially when it's off.

The screen will show you a message from someone you love.
It will look real.
It will sound real.
It is not.

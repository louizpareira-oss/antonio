# 2. The replacement workflow

Same dark, cinematic, philosophy aesthetic your 12.5K followers came for. Different
sourcing, so it clears the originality bar.

Pick **one** of the three routes below and commit to it for 30 days. Do not mix all three
at once — the account needs a consistent, recognizable identity.

---

## Route A — "Shot it myself" (safest, cheapest, slowest)

Highest approval odds. Nothing in the video came from anyone else.

**What you film:** you do not need to show your face. Shoot 10–15 second clips on your
phone, in slow motion where possible:

- walking at night, streetlights, headlights passing
- rain on a window, water, sea, sky, clouds moving
- your hands — writing in a notebook, pouring coffee, tying shoes, gym chalk
- your back walking away from camera, silhouette in a doorway
- an empty room, a made bed, a lamp, a mirror with fog
- your city — buildings, an empty street at 5am, a train window

**Build a library.** One hour of filming on a Sunday = 40–60 clips = 3 weeks of content.
Keep them in a folder, organized by mood: `calm/`, `tense/`, `lonely/`, `victory/`.
This library is an asset. Nobody can flag it, and nobody else has it.

**Voice:** record yourself. Your phone's voice memo app, under a blanket to kill echo,
is genuinely good enough. An accent is not a problem — it is the thing that makes it
yours. If you truly will not use your voice, see Route B.

**Why it works:** your footage + your voice + your script = 100% original. There is
nothing for a reviewer to object to.

---

## Route B — "I generated it myself" (fits your current look best)

Instead of downloading a cartoon GIF someone else made, you **generate** the visual.

**Tools:** any AI video/image generator you have access to (Sora, Runway, Kling, Luma,
Midjourney + an image-to-video pass, Leonardo). Free tiers are enough to start.

**How:** write a prompt that matches the line in your script.

> Script line: *"If you avoid conflict to keep the peace, you start a war inside yourself."*
>
> Prompt: *"Lone armored knight standing alone in an empty stone hall, dust in shafts of
> light, cinematic, shallow depth of field, moody, slow push-in, 9:16 vertical"*

Generate at **9:16 vertical, 1080×1920** natively. Never generate 4:3 and letterbox it.

**Rules that keep this original:**
- You wrote the prompts, you chose the takes, you composed the sequence — that is
  authorship. Downloading a Giphy GIF is not.
- Disclose AI-generated content using TikTok's built-in AI label. TikTok does **not**
  demonetize labelled AI visuals; it demonetizes undisclosed and unoriginal ones. Hiding
  the label is what gets accounts struck.
- Still add your own voice on top if you can. AI visuals + AI voice + borrowed quote is
  thin. AI visuals + **your** voice + **your** script is a real video.

This route is closest to the knight/armor aesthetic in your profile picture and your
current videos, so your audience will not feel the change as a break.

---

## Route C — "On camera" (highest payoff, hardest)

You, talking to the camera, 60–90 seconds, one idea per video, cinematic grade.
B-roll from your own library (Route A) cut over the middle third.

This monetizes best long-term because it builds a person, not a format. Faceless pages
plateau; people do not. But only take this route if you will actually do it weekly.

---

## The script — this is where the real change happens

Stop step 1 of the course. Do not search Instagram for popular quotes.

**Write from your own life.** The formula that works in your niche:

```
1. HOOK (0–3s)      One sentence. A specific, uncomfortable claim.
                    "You are not tired. You are avoiding something."
2. THE MOMENT (3–20s)  Something that actually happened to you. Small and specific.
                    Not "we all struggle." Rather: "Last winter I did not leave
                    my room for nine days and I told everyone I was busy."
3. THE TURN (20–45s)  What changed, or what you understood. The mechanism, not the slogan.
4. THE LANDING (45–65s) One line the viewer will repeat to someone else.
```

Specific beats profound. "I stopped answering my phone before 10am" outperforms
"discipline is freedom" every single time, because only one of them could only have
come from you.

**If you want to reference a philosopher** — Marcus Aurelius, Nietzsche, Seneca — that is
fine and legal. But quote one line and then spend 50 seconds on *your* reading of it.
The commentary is the original work. A quote read aloud over stock visuals is not.

---

## Editing (the part of the course you should keep)

Keep CapCut. Keep the instinct for matching a cut to the pause in the voiceover — that
part of the tutorial was genuinely correct and it is why your 2.1M video worked.

Change these:

| Course said | Do this instead |
|---|---|
| 4:3 aspect ratio | **9:16, 1080×1920, full bleed.** Fill the whole screen. |
| Export 4:3, then mask into 9:16 | Skip entirely. No frame, no rounded box, no black bars. |
| Heavy VHS filter | Light grade only — contrast, slight desaturation, subtle grain. It must still read as sharp, modern footage. |
| GIFs from Giphy/Tenor/Pinterest | Your library (A) or your generations (B). |
| 30–40 second videos | **65–90 seconds.** Creator Rewards pays only above 60s. Aim 70s so you never land at 59. |
| Captions in Apple Garamond | Captions are fine — keep them clean, high contrast, centered in the safe zone. |

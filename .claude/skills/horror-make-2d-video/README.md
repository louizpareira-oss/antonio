# aiTrends 2D Studio — HORROR WARNING EDITION
Turn any voiceover into a fully animated, cinematic **horror warning / survival protocol** video: black stick figures with glowing red or white rim light acting out uncanny "something is wrong" scenarios in moody 2.5D sets, giant face close-ups with dead eyes, freeze-frame "the moment before" shots, animated warning text, VHS glitch artifacts, scan lines, analog distortion, low-poly 3D orbit shots of empty rooms and dark streets, with generated tension music and unsettling sound effects.

Every frame is drawn by code and rendered frame by frame. No image or video AI is used, so characters stay consistent and text stays sharp. Visuals are invented for each script; the horror style stays the same.

**Format DNA:** "If [familiar thing behaves impossibly wrong]... [URGENT WARNING IN ALL CAPS]" — survival protocols for situations nobody trained you for.

## What you can say
- "Make a horror video from this voiceover": runs the whole pipeline.
- `/horror-new-video`: set up a video, timestamp the narration, plan the protocol shots.
- `/horror-video-styles`: see your own shots in 5 horror looks (ABYSS, BLOOD MOON, MIDNIGHT, NEON DREAD, VHS NIGHTMARE), pick one or design your own.
- `/horror-render-video`: build and check the scenes, preview, render the final video.
- `/horror-video-sound`: tension music moods, unsettling SFX, eerie ambience, final mix.

## Inputs and options
- **Voiceover** (any common audio format) — calm, deadpan narrator voice recommended.
- **Script:** optional. Timestamped (SRT/VTT/JSON/TXT) or plain (timed automatically). Without a script, the voiceover is transcribed.
- **16:9 or 9:16**, **720p / 1080p / 1440p / 4K**, optional burned-in captions.
- **Music:** generated by horror mood, your own track, or none. **SFX:** 48 built-in effects (13 horror-specific: static, whisper, creak, distant scream, low tones and eerie ambience beds), or your own files.

## First-time setup (automatic)
On first use, Claude copies the bundled studio into your project folder (`aitrends-2d-studio-horror/`) and installs:
- ffmpeg,
- Node.js packages (renderer, Three.js, fonts),
- a headless Chromium,
- Python's numpy, scipy and faster-whisper.

Whisper downloads its speech model on the first transcription. Roughly 1 GB of disk is needed in total.

## Render times
A small 2-core cloud machine renders 720p at about 2 frames per second (a 5-minute video takes ~1 hour). 1080p takes about 2x longer and 4K about 8x. A modern desktop is 3-5x faster.

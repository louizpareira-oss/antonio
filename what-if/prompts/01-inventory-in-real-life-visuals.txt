WHAT IF YOU HAD AN INVENTORY IN REAL LIFE? — IMAGE PROMPT PACK
45 scenes. Numbers match S01-S45 in scripts/01-inventory-in-real-life.md.
The full style rules are in what-if/02-style-guide.md.

THE LOOK
The same 3D look as the reference channel. Every character is a stick figure with a big white
ball for a head, a face drawn in simple black lines, thin white arms and white ball hands.
The style is toon-shaded with thin dark outlines, in simple low-poly sets lit in dusky purple
and pink with warm lamps. The only change is the outfit colors: our hero, Mustard, always
wears a MUSTARD-YELLOW HOODIE and DARK NAVY PANTS.

HOW TO USE
- Each prompt below is complete. Copy one line and paste it into your image generator.
- "--ar 16:9" is Midjourney syntax. In other generators, delete it and pick 16:9 in the settings.
- Do this first: upload what-if/img/model-sheet.png as the character reference, then
  generate REF-A to REF-C at the bottom of this file. Pick the best image, save it as
  hero-reference.png, and use it as the character reference for all 45 scenes.
- Everyone else ("round-head" in the prompts) has the same body. Give them other hoodie and
  pants colors, plus hair or hats, but never the mustard/navy combination. That belongs to the hero.
- The inventory grid, slot icons, counters and on-screen words are NOT in these prompts,
  because image generators garble small text and grids. Add them in your editor; each
  "EDIT:" line says what goes on top of that shot.
- For motion, run each image through image-to-video for a 3-5 second clip.
- Lighting by section: S01-S27 lavender daylight, S28-S38 dusky evening (S29 is a night shot),
  S39-S45 purple night.

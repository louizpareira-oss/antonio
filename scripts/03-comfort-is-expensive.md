# Script 03 — "Comfort Is Expensive"

**Length:** ~1:20 (139 words, ~145 wpm with deliberate pauses, plus a 2s silent hold)
**Format:** 1080 × 1920 · Blueprint: [`prompts/03-comfort-is-expensive-v2-3.txt`](../prompts/03-comfort-is-expensive-v2-3.txt)

## The idea underneath

Comfort feels free. It isn't free, it's financed. Every delay is a loan, and the bill
arrives later as an untrained body, an unlearned skill, an unsent message. Discomfort is
the cheap option: you pay a small price now instead of everything later.

## Voiceover (copy this)

```
Comfort is the most expensive thing you own.

It costs you nothing today. And it charges you for the rest of your life.

Every snooze button is a loan.

Every "I'll do it later" is interest.

And one day, the bill shows up.

It looks like the body you never trained.

The skill you never learned.

The message you never sent.

Here's the truth nobody tells you. Discomfort is cheap.

Ten minutes of cold water.

One awkward conversation.

One page, written badly, at six in the morning.

That's it. That's the whole price.

Pay a little now, or pay everything later.

The bill is coming either way. You just get to choose the currency.

So stop buying comfort.

Start paying in effort.

Because the person you want to become is waiting on the other side of the hard thing.
```

Blank lines are pauses (~1s each). Leave a real beat after "the bill shows up" and
after "Discomfort is cheap."

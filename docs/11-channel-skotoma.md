# Channel: SKOTOMA

**Name:** SKOTOMA. *Scotoma* is the medical word for a blind spot in your vision, from Greek *skotos*, "darkness".
The K spelling keeps the name out of the medical search results, the same trick as "Bradikinin".

**Handle:** `@Skotoma` (fallback `@SkotomaFiles`). Check it's free in YouTube Studio → Customization → Basic info.

**Series inside the videos:** NIGHT SUPPORT // CALL 001, 002, ... (the narrator is the Night Support operator).
The videos don't change: the channel is SKOTOMA, the show is NIGHT SUPPORT.

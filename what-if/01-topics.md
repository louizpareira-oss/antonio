# 10 "What If" topics

**Formula:** What If You + one extreme rule a viewer understands in under two seconds.
The 10 topics are split across the three kinds of rule that click best with gamers:
real-life game mechanics, body changes with a number in them, and social powers.

**Already used by the channel this format comes from. Don't reuse these:** heart beating 10x
faster, Creative Mode, respawning, being the only guy at a girls' school, seeing crushes.

## 1. Inventory in real life ★ script written

**Core rule:** You have a 36-slot inventory, like a survival game. You touch an object and
think "take", and it vanishes into a slot. Identical items stack up to 64. A pencil and a
couch each take up one slot. Inside the inventory, time stops. You can't store anything alive.

**Titles**
- What If You Had an Inventory in Real Life?
- What If You Could Carry 36 of ANYTHING?
- What If Your Pockets Had Infinite Space?

**Why it works:** Every gamer has hit "inventory full", so the rule needs no explanation.
The payoffs write themselves (moving house in seven slots, pizza that never goes cold), and
so does the turn: a person nobody can search is a person everybody wants to hire.
The hotbar UI floating at the edge of the screen is the thumbnail.

Full script: [`scripts/01-inventory-in-real-life.md`](scripts/01-inventory-in-real-life.md)

## 2. Everyone's health bar

**Core rule:** A green health bar floats above every person's head, including yours. It drops
when they're hurt, sick, exhausted or heartbroken, and it refills slowly when they rest.

**Titles**
- What If You Could See Everyone's Health Bar?
- What If You Knew Exactly How Much HP Everyone Had?
- What If You Could See Who's About to Break?

**Why it works:** It starts as a superpower: you dodge the kid with a full bar who wants to
fight, and you know your friend is sick before they say so. Then it gets heavy, because
you can see your grandpa's bar and you can't look away from it. That turn from funny to
gut-punch is what drives comments and shares. The thumbnail is a crowd with bars, one
of them flashing red.

## 3. XP for everything

**Core rule:** Every action earns XP. Brushing your teeth gets +2, a hard conversation gets
+150. Fill the bar and you level up, and you put a skill point into any real-life stat.

**Titles**
- What If You Got XP for Everything You Did?
- What If Real Life Had a Leveling System?
- What If You Could Level Up Your Brain?

**Why it works:** It's a self-improvement fantasy dressed as a game. The cost is the
grind. You start doing everything for the number: farming XP by saying "thank you" 400
times a day, and avoiding anything with no XP attached, like your friends. Gamers know
exactly what grinding does to you.

## 4. A minimap

**Core rule:** A round minimap sits in the corner of your vision. Everyone within 200 meters
is a dot: green for people who like you, grey for strangers, red for people who don't.

**Titles**
- What If You Had a Minimap in Real Life?
- What If You Could See Every Enemy Coming?
- What If You Knew Who Secretly Hated You?

**Why it works:** The first half is pure gamer fun: you never get surprised, and you win
hide-and-seek forever. The second half is social horror: a red dot sits at your lunch table
and it's your best friend. Thumbnail: a minimap with one red dot labelled "BFF".

## 5. Undo the last 10 seconds

**Core rule:** Press an invisible Ctrl+Z and the whole world rewinds 10 seconds. Only you
remember. It recharges after 60 seconds.

**Titles**
- What If You Could Undo the Last 10 Seconds?
- What If You Had Ctrl+Z in Real Life?
- What If You Could Take Back Anything You Said?

**Why it works:** Ten seconds is small enough to be believable and big enough to save you
from every cringe moment, so the viewer instantly imagines their own worst ones. The 60-second
cooldown is the dramatic engine. The disaster always comes 11 seconds too late, or in the
59 seconds between charges.

## 6. Grow 1 inch every time you lie

**Core rule:** Every lie, even a tiny one, makes you 1 inch taller, permanently. White lies
count. "I'm fine" counts.

**Titles**
- What If You Grew 1 Inch Every Time You Lied?
- What If Lying Made You Taller?
- What If You Were 9 Feet Tall Because You Lied?

**Why it works:** It flips a fairy tale everyone knows, and the payoff is real: who doesn't
want to be taller? Then the math kicks in. The average person tells a lie or two a day, so
by the end of the year you're hitting doorframes, and everyone knows exactly how dishonest
you are just by looking at you. Number + body change + visible consequence.

## 7. Living at 2x speed

**Core rule:** Your body and brain run at 2x speed. To you, everyone else moves in slow
motion. To them, you're a blur. Your body also ages twice as fast.

**Titles**
- What If You Lived at 2x Speed?
- What If Everyone Else Was in Slow Motion?
- What If You Had 2x Speed in Real Life?

**Why it works:** "2x speed" is how this audience already watches videos, so it feels
personal. Payoffs: untouchable at sports, finishing homework in half the time, gaming
reflexes nobody can match. The cost is the hidden clause, aging twice as fast, which the
script holds back until the midpoint.

## 8. Your phone battery is your battery

**Core rule:** Your energy is your phone's battery level. At 100% you're unstoppable. At 20%
you're exhausted. At 1%, you have to find a charger or you pass out where you stand.

**Titles**
- What If Your Phone Battery Was YOUR Battery?
- What If You Passed Out at 0%?
- What If Your Phone Controlled Your Energy?

**Why it works:** It's the most relatable rule on the list. Every viewer has felt the 1%
panic. The comedy is easy: fast-charging your way through a test, someone stealing your
charger. The dark-humor turn: what happens when your phone falls in a lake?

## 9. Everyone hears your thoughts for 24 hours

**Core rule:** For exactly 24 hours, everyone within 10 meters hears your thoughts out loud,
in your voice. You can't turn it off.

**Titles**
- What If Everyone Could Hear Your Thoughts for 24 Hours?
- What If Your Thoughts Played Out Loud?
- What If You Couldn't Hide a Single Thought?

**Why it works:** The audience is self-conscious by definition, so this rule is a nightmare
they can feel. The timer gives the video a built-in structure (hour 1, hour 6, hour 23).
The ending can flip it: the only thought that slips out at the end is the nice one you
never said.

## 10. Only 100 words a day

**Core rule:** You get 100 spoken words a day. A counter floats in front of you. At 0, your
voice is gone until midnight. Written words don't count, but nobody else knows that.

**Titles**
- What If You Could Only Say 100 Words a Day?
- What If Every Word Cost You Something?
- What If You Ran Out of Words?

**Why it works:** The counter is the hook, because the viewer starts counting the
narrator's words. It's funny early (you speak like a caveman to save words) and lands
hard late (you used 99 words arguing about nothing, and have one left when it matters).
That ending is ready-made for comments: "What would your last word be?"

## Order to make them in

1. **Inventory** (1) — the clearest rule and the easiest to animate. Start here.
2. **Health bar** (2) — has the strongest emotional turn. Make it second to test whether the
   audience wants heavier endings.
3. **Grow 1 inch per lie** (6) — the best thumbnail on the list.
4. Then follow your analytics: if the game-mechanic videos win, do 3, 4 and 5 next. If the
   social ones win, do 9 and 10.

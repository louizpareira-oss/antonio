## What is the same in all of them (facts, not guesses)
- **Exactly 3 hashtags at the top** (the ones YouTube shows above the title), always including #whatif and #animation,
  plus one topic tag (#superpower, #genius, #IQ…).
- **The first line is the hook:** the title question, or "X sounds amazing. Until…". It is what shows in search
  and above "Show more".
- **One specific, easy comment question** ("Where would you go first?", "Who do you think the villain is?"), not a
  vague "what do you think?".
- **No keyword stuffing.** The two IFOLOGY videos (166K in a day, 583K) are under 40 words.

## What some of them add
- A **📺 link to another video** of the channel (IFOLOGY) or a playlist, to keep the viewer watching the channel.
- A **series hook**: "This is Part 1 of 3", "Part 2 is coming".
- A one-line **brand tagline** at the end ("Every story starts with IF.").
- **Chapters** (the 1M video has them).

## Honest note
Length doesn't decide the views: tiny and medium descriptions both reached 500K–1M, and the long one with sources has
the fewest views (but it was only a day old). The description's job is the first two lines, the comment question and
the link to the next video; the title and thumbnail do the rest.

## Our template (used from video 3 on)
1. 3 hashtags: `#whatif #animation #superpower` (or the topic tag).
2. The title question, then 2–3 short "you" lines with a "Sounds amazing. Until…" turn.
3. "This is Part 1." when a sequel is set up.
4. One specific "Be honest…" comment question.
5. `📺 Watch next:` + the title of our previous video, with its link pasted on the next line.
6. Chapters (each ≥ 10 s).
7. One line of real science (optional).
8. Sign-off: "Parallel You: every what if, lived by you." + subscribe + business email.
9. 4 hashtags at the end: the 3 from the top + #parallelyou.

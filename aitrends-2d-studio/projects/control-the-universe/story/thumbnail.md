# Thumbnail — Can One Person Really Control the Universe?

Three original concepts for the user to generate in SeaArt (rules in `CLAUDE.md`; lessons and the new reference look
in `docs/13-thumbnail-patterns.md`). Each prompt starts with the studio's required block, word for word, then
describes the blue + red superhero suit from this video. The user adds the 2-word text.

**SeaArt negative prompt (all three):**
`3d render, octane, photorealistic, realistic texture, wide shot, tiny character, full street, cross-section, human skin, realistic face, thick body, chibi, Pixar, text, letters, numbers, logo, watermark, signs, clutter, small objects, extra limbs, gauntlet, gold glove, Marvel`

**Pick:** upload all three with YouTube's Test & compare. If only one: **#1 ALL SIX.** (it shows the power itself).

---

## 1. "ALL SIX." — six stones over his glove, the city frozen behind him
```
16:9 aspect ratio, YouTube thumbnail. 2D cel-shaded cartoon illustration style with bold black outlines, flat vibrant colors, and dramatic comic-book glow. A slender stick-figure with thin limbs, a solid black spherical head, and glowing white eyes, wearing a bright blue hoodie. Close-up: the stick-figure is huge in the foreground, taking up 50-60% of the frame, his facial expression big and readable on a phone screen. High contrast, dramatic cyan and neon orange glow. Strictly NO human skin, NO realistic faces, NO thick/chibi bodies, NO 3D render, NO realistic textures.
His blue hoodie has become a superhero suit: a red V-shaped shoulder panel and red sleeves, red gloves, a gold belt, a flowing red cape with a gold lining, and a glowing gold hexagonal badge on his chest set with six small colored crystals. Close-up on the right side of the frame: he thrusts one open red-gloved palm at the camera, big and foreshortened, and six glowing hexagonal crystals (gold, cyan, orange-red, violet, green, pink) float in a ring above it, crackling with light. He gives a calm, sly half-closed side-eye and a small smirk, not angry. Behind him the world is frozen: a coffee splash hangs in mid-air and a pigeon is stuck mid-flap, with cyan time-ripple rings and bold comic-book impact lines. Simple flat sunrise sky in orange and pink with an orange glow right behind his head so its round outline stands out. The left 40% of the frame is clean flat sky, empty space for text. No text, no letters, no numbers, no signs.
```
**Text:** **ALL** (white) / **SIX.** (yellow). **Optional extra:** a red arrow to the six stones.

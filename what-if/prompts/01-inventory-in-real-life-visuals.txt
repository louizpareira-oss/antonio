WHAT IF YOU HAD AN INVENTORY IN REAL LIFE? — IMAGE PROMPT PACK
45 scenes. Numbers match S01-S45 in scripts/01-inventory-in-real-life.md.

HOW TO USE
- Each prompt below is complete. Copy one line and paste it into your image generator.
- "--ar 16:9" is Midjourney syntax. In other generators, delete it and pick 16:9 in the settings.
- Generate S04 first. Pick the best Blob and use it as the character reference
  for every other scene, so Blob looks the same in all 45.
- Every other person in the video is also a white blob, told apart by clothes and props.
- The inventory grid, slot icons, counters and on-screen words are NOT in these prompts,
  because image generators garble small text and grids. Add them in your editor; each
  "EDIT:" line says what goes on top of that shot.
- For motion, run each image through image-to-video for a 3-5 second clip.

CHARACTER (already included in every prompt):
Cute 3D blob character: small round soft white body, simple face, big expressive glossy black eyes, tiny green leaf sprout on top of its head, wearing an oversized mustard-yellow hoodie

STYLE (already included in every prompt):
clean minimalist animation style, bright vibrant colors, soft lighting, high quality, no text --ar 16:9

Change the hoodie color or the leaf sprout if you like, but then change it in EVERY prompt.
The sprout is there on purpose. It makes Blob your character instead of a copy of
someone else's.

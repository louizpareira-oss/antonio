# Stairwell Lights — narration script

Spoken text is plain. Lines starting with ▸ are production directions and are not spoken.


## Cold open

▸ *SC01: Anomaly first: a light wakes two floors above anyone.*
Floor four just turned on.
You're still on floor one.

▸ *SC02: Introduce the sensor: the 'eye' that will track everything.*
These lights only wake for movement.

▸ *SC03: Establish normal behavior and YOU's position below.*
One floor at a time, as you climb.
Nobody is on four.

▸ *SC04: State the core prohibition (Rule 1).*
Rule one.
Don't climb into a light you didn't turn on.

▸ *SC05: Name the automatic reaction, then stop it.*
You'll want to call up the stairs.
Just a quiet "hello?" Don't.

▸ *SC06: Distinguish from the ordinary explanation.*
A neighbor's light would climb with them, floor by floor.
This one stays on four.

▸ *SC07: Hold on the contradiction.*
No footsteps.
No door opening.
Nobody comes to the rail.

▸ *SC08: First movement: it comes one floor toward YOU.*
Twenty seconds later, four goes dark.
And three turns on.


## Title

▸ *SC09: Title.*


## How the lights work

▸ *SC10: Plant: YOU's mailbox 5B; an older name label shows under YOU's.*

▸ *SC11: Teach the tracker rule (LED = motion seen).*
Every landing has a sensor above its light.
It blinks green when something moves beneath it.

▸ *SC12: Teach the timer and the stillness principle (seeds the ending).*
Twenty seconds of stillness, and the light goes out.
Still things don't keep a light on.

▸ *SC13: Teach the threat's movement grammar.*
Whatever lit four moves only in the dark.
One floor at a time.
Toward the newest movement it can find.
When nothing moves, it goes back to four.


## Rule 2

▸ *SC14: Rule 2 heading.*
Rule two.

▸ *SC15: Normal habit setup.*
Your light will go out on you.
It always does, about halfway up the stairs.

▸ *SC16: Name the micro-habit and stop it mid-action.*
You know the move.
Arms up.
A little wave.
The light comes back.
Not tonight.

▸ *SC17: Teach the trigger signal for Rule 2.*
When your light dims, freeze.
The sensor turns amber first.
You get two seconds.

▸ *SC18: Obey the rule in darkness.*
Then dark.

▸ *SC19: Consequence: obeying makes it lose YOU; it returns home.*
Three stays lit above you.
Nothing new moves.
Twenty seconds.
Three goes dark.
Then four turns on again.

▸ *SC20: Plant: four is its home.*
It went back.
Four is where it waits.
It follows movement, not you.


## Rule 3

▸ *SC21: Rule 3 heading.*
Rule three.

▸ *SC22: Spatial goal and obstacle.*
Your apartment is on five, the floor right above it.
Four is in the way.

▸ *SC23: Micro-habit (rushing) stopped.*
Scared people take stairs two at a time.
Don't.

▸ *SC24: Teach the slow-climb technique.*
These sensors miss slow movement.
One step.
One breath.
Another step.

▸ *SC25: Plant: something rested on three's rail when it stood there.*
On three, the dust on the rail is wiped clean in one spot.
The size of a palm.

▸ *SC26: Set the trigger signal.*
If the green eye above you blinks, it saw you.

▸ *SC27: A slip: YOU moved too fast.*
Stop.

▸ *SC28: Hold under pressure (time compressed).*
Let the light run out before you move again.
Twenty seconds is a long time to hold a breath.

▸ *SC29: Consequence of the slip: it moves toward the blink.*
Three turns on.
It came one floor closer to the blink.

▸ *SC30: Obedience works; narrator approval (complicity seed).*
Hold.
Twenty more seconds.
Three goes dark, and four lights up again.
Good.


## Rule 4

▸ *SC31: Rule 4 heading.*
Rule four.

▸ *SC32: Micro-habit: keys as a weapon.*
Your keys are in your fist.
One between each finger.
Everyone does that.

▸ *SC33: Problem: small motion and sound.*
They swing when you climb.
They chime against each other.

▸ *SC34: Consequence shown: it reacts to the chime.*
It notices small movement, even in the dark.

▸ *SC35: Obey: physical action.*
Set them down on the step.
Slowly.
No sound.

▸ *SC36: Plant: keys left behind.*
You can come back for them in the morning.
Your door key is the brass one, on top.


## Rule 5

▸ *SC37: Rule 5 heading.*
Rule five.

▸ *SC38: Pressure: fatigue.*
Your legs are shaking by floor three.
You'll want to stop on a landing.
Flat floor.
A wall to lean on.

▸ *SC39: Teach blind zones (mechanism).*
But the sensors watch the landings.
Not the steps between them.

▸ *SC40: Narrator slip (midpoint).*
Rest on the fourth landing until it's quiet.

▸ *SC41: Correction; seeds the reveal.*
Below it.
I meant the steps below it.

▸ *SC42: New viewpoint on four.*
From the steps, four is at eye level.

▸ *SC43: Plant: someone stood on four for a long time.*
There are two worn patches in its floor.
Shoe-shaped.
Facing the wall.
Someone stood there long enough to wear through the paint.


## Rule 6

▸ *SC44: Rule 6 heading.*
Rule six.

▸ *SC45: Escalating exception arrives.*
The lobby door opens below you.
A neighbor, home late, with a grocery bag.

▸ *SC46: Cause of the all-lights state.*
They press the orange button by the mailboxes.

▸ *SC47: New state: all fixtures on.*
Every light comes on at once.
One minute, every floor.

▸ *SC48: Visible proof without showing a creature.*
Now you can see four.
Nobody is standing on it.
But the light throws a shadow on the worn patches.

▸ *SC49: Temptation: proof that light is safe.*
Your neighbor walks right past it.
Their bag swings against their leg.
Nothing happens.
The lights were on.
They kept moving.

▸ *SC50: Make the threat's indifference to light-time movement concrete.*
They don't look up at four.
People never do.

▸ *SC51: Pressure.*
You'll want to run now.
Past four.
Home.

▸ *SC52: Rule 6 + mechanism.*
Don't run while every light is on.
The timer ends on every floor at once.

▸ *SC53: Stakes of running.*
And whatever is moving then is the newest thing in the dark.
You would be halfway up the flight.

▸ *SC54: Newest movement = six.*
Your neighbor's door closes on six.

▸ *SC55: All dark at once.*
The minute ends.

▸ *SC56: Escalation: it's at YOU's door.*
Then five turns on.
Your floor.
Nobody is on five.


## Rule 7

▸ *SC57: Rule 7 heading.*
Rule seven.

▸ *SC58: Threat at home.*
It's outside your door.

▸ *SC59: Micro-habit: hurrying home.*
When you see your own floor, you'll want to hurry.
Don't.

▸ *SC60: The trap disguised as a rule (complicity).*
When nothing moves, it goes back to four.
But it can't stand where someone's already standing.

▸ *SC61: YOU obeys.*
Take four before it does.
Step onto the landing slowly, the way you climbed.
Face the wall, and hold still.

▸ *SC62: Payoff of the worn patches (clue 1).*
Your feet fit the worn patches.

▸ *SC63: It moves.*
Five goes dark.

▸ *SC64: Approach, heard not seen.*
Something comes down the stairs.
Slowly.
One step, one breath, another step.

▸ *SC65: Shadow moving against established light.*
Four turns on as it passes.
Its shadow crosses the wall in front of you.
Don't turn around.

▸ *SC66: Payoff of the keys (clue 2).*
It stops on the step below you.
Your keys lift off the step.

▸ *SC67: Replacement: it goes home as YOU.*
Then it climbs back up.
Five turns on.
Your door unlocks.
Your door closes.

▸ *SC68: The swap condition.*
Four is still on.
Stand still.
Twenty seconds.


## Ending

▸ *SC69: YOU is now a still thing.*
You can stop holding your breath now.
It won't matter.
The sensor can't see you anymore.

▸ *SC70: Reveal: the narrator was the one on four.*
Every rule kept you still.
Every rule kept you in the dark.
Thank you for standing still.
I stood there for years.
The patches are mine.

▸ *SC71: Explain the cycle that produces the opening image.*
When someone new walks in, you'll turn toward them.
You won't mean to.
Four will light up, and they'll see it.

▸ *SC72: Payoff of the mailbox (clue 3).*

▸ *SC73: Reinterpreting final image (mirror of SC01).*
Floor four just turned on.

▸ *SC74: Closing callback.*
Don't climb into a light you didn't turn on.

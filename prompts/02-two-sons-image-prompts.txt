This file is only for generating the 16 still frames. The motion
prompts are in 02-two-sons-animation-prompts.txt - do not open it

HOW TO USE
- Paste each prompt into a still generator at 9:16 vertical
  (add --ar 9:16 on Midjourney). Generate 4, keep the best one.
- Name the file you keep after the clip number: 01.png, 02.png,
  ... 16.png. Stage 2 refers to them by number.
- Do the MATCHED PAIRS back to back and feed the approved still
  of the first one in as a character/style reference on the
  second, so the framing and the characters carry over:
      1  ->  15 -> 16   (same room, same camera height, 3 times)
      4  ->  6          (the two schools)
      5  ->  7          (the two arrivals)
      8  ->  10         (the two classrooms)
      9  ->  11         (the two close-ups)
- Nothing here describes movement on purpose. These are frozen
  moments. Do not add "slow motion", "push in" or "camera tracks"
  to an image prompt - it comes back blurred.

CHARACTER BIBLE (keep these words identical in every prompt)
Every character is a HUMAN BODY with a PIGEON HEAD - fully
clothed, standing upright on two human legs, human arms and
human hands. Feathers and beak on the head only. No wings, no
bird feet, no full-bird characters anywhere in this video.
  POP-CUH     - grown man's body, slate grey pigeon head, gold
                motorcycle helmet, heavy gold chains over a
                black tracksuit
  GOLDEN SON  - schoolboy's body, gleaming iridescent gold-dipped
                pigeon head, gold-dipped skin, small gold helmet,
                gold chain, gold school uniform
  POOPY SON   - schoolboy's body, matted brown pigeon head,
                bright alert intelligent eyes, plastic bottle cap
                helmet, mud-stained hand-me-down clothes

TWO PALETTES, never mixed until clip 14
  GOLD  - over-lit, lens flare, warm luxurious amber, pristine
  BROWN - flat overcast light, drab muddy brown and grey

NEGATIVE PROMPT (paste into the negative field for every still)

limbs, deformed hands, distorted face, human head, human face,
feathered arms, wings instead of arms, full bird body, bird

horizontal, letterbox, black bars, split screen, collage,

## Channel description (Customization → Profile → Description)
Big questions. Simple answers.

Can one person really control the universe? Can 2 people really restart humanity? Could you survive on Mars, stop time, or fall into a black hole?

Simple Science takes the wildest "can it really happen?" questions and answers them with a short animated story and the real science behind it. Every video is about 4 minutes: one impossible idea, followed all the way, then checked against what scientists actually know.

Space, physics, the human body, superpowers and the future. No boring lectures, just big questions made simple.

New videos are always on the way. Subscribe so you don't miss the next one.

Got a question you want answered? Drop it in the comments. The best ones become videos.

WHY TWO STAGES
Text-to-video redraws the characters every clip and they drift -
by clip 9 the bottle cap helmet is a hat. Generate a STILL for
each clip, approve it, then feed that exact still into an
image-to-video tool as the first frame. The characters stay
identical because they are literally the same pixels.

HOW TO USE
- Stage 1: paste the IMAGE > prompt into a still generator at
  9:16 (add --ar 9:16 on Midjourney). Make 4, keep the best.
- Stage 2: upload that still as the first frame, paste the
  ANIMATE > prompt as the motion description. 5 seconds, or 3 on
  the clips marked (3s).
- Do the MATCHED PAIRS back to back and reuse the approved still
  as a style/character reference on the second one:
      1  ->  15 -> 16   (same room, same camera height, three times)
      4  ->  6          (the two schools)
      5  ->  7          (the two arrivals)
      8  ->  10         (the two classrooms)
      9  ->  11         (the two close-ups)
  For 15 and 16, reuse the clip 1 still as the reference image so
  the room, window line and camera height match exactly.
- Never regenerate an approved still. If the video take is bad,
  re-run the ANIMATE prompt on the same still.
- Turn ON TikTok's AI-generated content label when you upload.

STYLE TAIL (already inside every IMAGE prompt below)

IMAGE NEGATIVE PROMPT
text, watermark, logo, subtitles, captions, signature, extra
limbs, deformed wings, distorted face, photorealistic, live
action, horror, horizontal, letterbox, black bars, split screen,
collage, cluttered composition

ANIMATION RULES (the difference between a clip and a mess)
- One action per clip. Name it, then name what must NOT move.
- Say "camera completely locked" on every static shot. Video
  models add drift and push-in unless you forbid it.
- Ask for small motion. Big motion is where faces melt.
- Liquids: always say heavy, thick, slow, viscous. Default
  physics look like water and kill the joke.
- Blinks and one small head move read as "alive" without risking
  the face. Use them instead of performance.
ANIMATION NEGATIVE PROMPT
morphing, warping face, changing costume, character swap, extra
characters appearing, camera shake, handheld, fast motion, zoom,
text, watermark, subtitles

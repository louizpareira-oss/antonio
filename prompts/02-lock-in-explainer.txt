HOW THIS PACK WORKS
- Model: Nano Banana 2, 1K, 9:16 (768x1376). 20 credits per image.
- Shots marked [REF] use image2image with SHOT 01 as the character
  reference, so Leo looks the same everywhere. Other shots are text-only.
- Kinetic text is added in the edit: EVERY spoken word, one at a time,
  huge bold rounded sans, dead center, rotating colors (lime, cream,
  yellow, coral, blue). Never generated into the image.
- Animate only on the owner's go, one shot at a time.

CHARACTER — LEO (from SHOT 01)
Young man, curly dark hair, big expressive cartoon eyes, thick
eyebrows. Same outfit and proportions as SHOT 01 in every [REF] shot.

STYLE SUFFIX (appended to every prompt below)
Vibrant 2D flat vector cartoon illustration, polished premium
animated-explainer YouTube style, 9:16 vertical portrait, bold and
full of energy. Highly saturated colors, clean geometric shapes,
smooth flat fills with soft gradient shading and rim light, crisp
shapes, high contrast, dynamic composition, rich background detail.
Main subject in the lower two thirds, upper middle area slightly
calmer for a big caption. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos,
no watermark, no photorealism, no 3D render.

REF PREFIX (prepended to every [REF] prompt)
The same cartoon character as the reference image: the young man
with curly dark hair, big expressive eyes and thick eyebrows, same
outfit, same art style and colors.

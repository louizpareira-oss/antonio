loudness, silence detection, PocketSphinx word count (checked on our own video: it counted 748 words against the

listened to.

## Story structure

**A (8:56)**
- 0:00–0:17 cold open: impossible things in close-up (31-day battery, full signal three floors underground).
- 0:17 title card.
- 0:19 one real fact as a graph: an IQ bell curve (100 average, 140 rare, tests stop around 160).
- 0:30–3:45 life with the power, with a gold **+** (top-left) or grey **−** (top-right) icon on screen for good and bad
  sides. Small labels everywhere ("LEAK · TODAY", "100% UNHACKABLE", "$260,080 · 7 DAYS").
- He hides it: he scores exactly 100 on purpose, because "nobody's looking for you, that's the real superpower".
- 3:45–4:30 his mom's backstory: two jobs, saved for his university, kept every drawing.
- 4:40–6:00 mom loses her savings to a fake "bank fraud team" call.
- 6:00–7:45 he finds the call centre (40 phones, 300 victims), sends evidence, gets arrests and 300 refunds.
- 7:45–8:15 payoff: a callback to an early downside (he never laughed on time; now he does).
- 8:15–8:40 twist, typed on his screen: "NICE WORK, LEO. THAT CALL CENTER WAS ONE OF OURS. … WE'VE BEEN LOOKING FOR YOU
  FOR YEARS."

**B (7:46)**
- 0:00–0:16 cold open: people get hurt and don't react; a penalty kick.
- 0:17 "6 MONTHS AGO": the ZERO shot (one shot, pain gone for good) was made by copying your body.

  visits. "REAL" fact boxes appear during the story ("About 1 in 7 adults is afraid of the dentist").
- 2:45 villain: a black car and a ZERO salesman want your blood every week on a lifetime contract. You say no.
- 3:15–4:30 his little sister has ZERO in her: a bandage she didn't notice, then a 102 °F fever, then appendicitis
  nobody can see.

- 6:00–6:55 "Is any of this real?": real people born without pain, the SCN9A gene, an FDA approval in Jan 2025.
- 7:00–7:41 twist: in the lab his body takes no damage and his cells show 0 % aging. "Would you take ZERO? Tell us in

**What A and B both do that ours doesn't**
1. A **family member** with an emotional backstory before the danger. Ours has Mia, a friend seen in 2 scenes.
2. The hero **pays a lasting price** to save them: a lifetime contract, or being found by an organization. Ours only
   falls on the sidewalk.
3. A **villain or organization** connected to the power.
4. **World-scale effects** of the power, not just personal tricks (B most of all).
5. A **twist in the last 30 s that opens a sequel** ("Part 2", "To be continued").
6. Time spent on the emotional story: A about 4 min (3:45–8:00), B about 3 min (2:45–5:55), ours about 1 min
   (2:36–3:41).
7. Real facts shown inside the story ("REAL" boxes, the IQ curve), not only in an end section.

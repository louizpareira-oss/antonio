# Story bible — What If You Could Talk to Animals?

**Logline:** One morning you understand every animal, and there's no off switch. It's the best thing ever, then the
dawn chorus, the crows and the pet shop make it a curse, and it gives you one last year with Biscuit, your
14-year-old dog. The day the birds go silent, the whales answer.

**Look (v2, after the user's Oct 21 references):** bright, clean flat colours, `clean` grade; characters in real
clothes with big eyes (`scenes/look.js`); animals in natural colours (`scenes/animals.js`); talking = white speech
bubbles that pop next to the animal (only the hero hears them). Big faces and close-ups often; a new picture every 3–4 s.

**Cast (one signature colour each):**
- YOU: blue hoodie `#2E72D2`, white sneakers.
- BISCUIT: old yellow Lab, white face, red collar + gold tag.
- PICKLES: black-and-white tuxedo cat, green eyes, heavy lids.
- MOM: green sweater, bun.  THEO: orange T-shirt + orange cap.  VET: teal scrubs.
- Animals: crows (blue sheen), pigeon (grey, green neck), red squirrel (key), prairie dogs (sandy), robins, puppies,
  rabbits, vervet monkeys, bees, elephants, sperm whales.

**Locations:** the house by the sea (kitchen with blue cabinets, living room with the sunny spot and Biscuit's red bed,
bedroom, stairs), street with the bakery, park and dog park, zoo, school hall and cafeteria, Happy Paws pet shop,
gas station at night in the rain (flashback), City Vet, city rooftops at dusk, beach at sunset, the sea at dawn,
underwater, blueprint science, Kenya savanna, Arizona desert.

**Motifs:** the OFF switch (never works; dusty at the end) · 4:47 on the clock · Biscuit's sunny spot · 41 pieces of
bacon · the sea window with a far whale tail · "MY TREE!".

**Music plan:** playful (hook, fun part) → warm (Biscuit's story) → tension/comic (curse) → wonder (science) →
mystery (whale recording) → sad (Biscuit) → mystery/tension (twist) → hopeful (end).

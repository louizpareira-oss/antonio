# Story bible — What If You Got 2x Heavier Every Single Day?

**Logline:** Every midnight your weight doubles while your body keeps up. The world can't carry you. You lose the lake
and the bridge to Grandpa, and on day 10 you leave Earth for good, where you finally float again.

**Cast (signature colours):**
- YOU: blue hoodie `#2E72D2`.
- GRANDPA: retired lifeguard, red jersey with white trim, white visor, white moustache.
- MOM: green.
- FRIEND: orange cap and tee.
- FRIEND'S DAD: olive, with a pickup.
- DR. VOSS: lab coat, teal lanyard, Project Atlas `#3FC1C9`.
- Extras: COACH (red tracksuit), WORKERS (orange, hard hats), KIDS (mixed colours).

**Locations and mood (style `golden`, bright picture):**
- **Bathroom / living / bedroom:** sunny morning, warm.
- **Gym and school hall:** bright, cool white.
- **Lake:** the flashback is sepia. The present is a hard summer noon. Underwater is teal with light rays.
- **Street:** golden; footprints and steel plates.
- **Garage:** a warm work lamp at night, then cold Atlas floodlights.
- **River and bridge:** golden on day 9, dawn mist on day 10, night with the porch light at the end.
- **Launch pad:** night under floodlights. The capsule interior is soft white and blue. Orbit shows a big blue Earth.
- **Blueprint science:** bright navy with a glow.

**Motifs:**
- The top-left weight badge (DAY · kg/t).
- Midnight clocks with a ×2 stamp.
- The weight seesaw (gorilla → piano → horse → car → T. rex → elephant → fire truck → 18-wheeler → Titanic).
- The 10 t sign.
- Floating with arms spread: little you, then you in orbit.
- "You still floating, kiddo?"

**Music plan:**
- tension (hook) → playful (rules) → hopeful/sad (lake) → playful/tension (house) → sad (bridge)
- → mystery/tension (Atlas) → hopeful (river) → epic (launch) → wonder (float)
- → wonder (science) → mystery/tension (twist) → epic (end).

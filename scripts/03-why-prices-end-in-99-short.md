## Why 68 seconds and not exactly 60

Your notes (`docs/01-why-the-appeal-failed.md`) say Creator Rewards only pays on videos **longer than
1 minute**. A script that aims at 60.0 seconds lands under the line the moment you speak a little
faster than planned or trim a pause in the edit. So this cut is built to land at 66 to 70 seconds.

- If your recording comes in **under 64 seconds**, you are rushing. Slow down, do not add words.
- If you want it a little tighter, cut the single line "You read left to right." That saves about
  3 seconds and you still land near 65 seconds.
- Do not cut more than that.

## The voiceover script

**Record this in your own voice**, straight through. Each line break is a breath. Sleeve is dry and
teasing: say "You said three ninety-nine." with a grin.

```
Which is cheaper: four dollars, or three ninety-nine?

You said three ninety-nine.

It's one cent.

So why does it feel like a discount?

Let's look in the hat.

The popular story says odd prices forced cashiers to open the drawer.

Nobody has proved it. Here's what has been.

You read left to right.

Three ninety-nine? Your brain files it as three-something.

One cent. A whole category.

In one catalogue test, a dress sold more after its price rose to end in nine.

The higher price won.

So the tag isn't telling you the price.

It's telling you how to feel about it.

Next time, round it up in your head.

Still want it?

Then buy it. Your choice, not the tag's.

Follow for one hidden habit a day.
```

**Word count:** 128. "Follow for one hidden habit a day." is a placeholder: replace it with whatever your account really
promises.

## Pacing and edit rules

- **Speech:** about 109 words a minute *including* pauses. Silence after the punchlines (shots 3, 21,
  29) does real work. Do not fill it.
- **Shots:** about 3 seconds each, with a slow camera move inside every shot. Never a still image.
- **Captions:** 1 to 3 words at a time, white with a dark outline, in the lower third. Type the
  numbers ($3.99, $4.00) as **gold** text.
- **Full bleed:** 1080 × 1920 from the first frame. No boxes, no bars, no title strip.
- **Portal:** hold black for 0.3 s at the end of shot 5, then start shot 8.
- **Label it:** switch on TikTok's AI-generated-content label when you upload.

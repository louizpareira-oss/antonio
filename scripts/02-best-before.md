## Title options

2. **Who Wrote the Date on Your Food?**
3. **You're Throwing Away Good Food**

## Central idea

"Best before" is about quality, not safety, but many people read it as "bad after" and bin
good food. "Use by" is the date that is about safety.

## Claims that need their qualification kept

- **"Still safe"** only when the food has been stored as the pack says and the packaging is
  intact. That's why the line says "Stored right". Food with visible mould or damage is not
  covered.
- **4.4 million tonnes** is WRAP's figure for 2022 (it was 4.7 million in 2021/22), so the line
  says "about".
- **"Hundreds of fruit and veg"**, not "all food". Products with "use by" dates keep them.
- The episode is about **UK** labels. Other countries use different wording, so keep "UK" in
  the lines and captions.

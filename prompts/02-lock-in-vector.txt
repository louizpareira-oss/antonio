VOICEOVER (230 words · read at ~145 wpm, firm, not shouted)

Lock in. I don't know how many times I need to tell you this. Lock in.
You're running out of time.
All you have done is scroll, scroll, and scroll. The year's almost
over, and you have wasted every single minute of it.
What — do you think some magical fairy is going to turn up and fix
your life? Genuinely? Because that's how you're acting.
Lock in. You're starting today, right now. Start taking steps towards
the goal. It doesn't matter if they are big or they are small — just
start moving. Slow motion is better than no motion.
We are running out of time. You weren't put on this earth to just sit
there.
You keep telling yourself, "Oh yeah, I'll start tomorrow… or next
week" — acting like your youth is a permanent setting. It's not. You
are actively burning the best years of your life.
Now, don't get me wrong. If you're young, just enjoy your childhood.
But for the rest of you… you're running out of time.
But look at me. The year isn't fully gone yet. There are still
minutes left on the clock. You can still turn this around.
Or you can choose to let the rest of this year bleed out.
So what's it going to be? Break the loop today. Close the apps today.
You have to take control. This is your life.

LENGTH NOTE: about 1:58 total. Well over the 60s Creator Rewards
minimum. If retention drops off in testing, the safest cut is

HOW TO USE
- Paste each "Motion Prompt" into your AI video tool at 9:16
  VERTICAL, 1080x1920. Never accept a horizontal or letterboxed
  result. Do not crop a wide video to fit.
- Paste the NEGATIVE PROMPT below into the negative field for
  every scene.
- Generate 4-5s per scene, then trim to the timestamps in CapCut.
  The action happens in the first ~1s; the rest is idle hold.
  Scenes under 2s (1, 3, 9, 19) need a trim to the first ~2s.
- Kinetic words and SFX are added in the edit, NOT generated. Keep
  the generated clips text-free.
- Lock the SEED where your tool has one, so recurring objects
  (padlock, hourglass, year ring, phone) look the same every time.
- Turn ON TikTok's AI-generated content label when you upload.

THE VOICE IS URGENT, THE PICTURES STAY CALM
The script pushes hard. The visuals do not speed up to match it.
Locked camera, one slow action per scene, soft crossfades. The
contrast is the point: a loud voice over still, deliberate images
reads as conviction, not noise.

two-thirds or around the center, so each word lands over a glow
halo and never covers the subject's one action.

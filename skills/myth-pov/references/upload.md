# Upload rules (YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Reels)

## Title (≤ 60 characters, the hook, second person or a sharp question)
- "You Can't Be Hurt… Except by One Plant" · "You Chained Death. Now Nobody Dies." · "Your Heart vs a Feather"
- No clickbait the video doesn't pay off. No ALL CAPS sentences (1 capitalised word max). 1 emoji max, optional.

## Description (Shorts)
Line 1: one sentence that restates the hook in new words. Line 2: exactly 3 hashtags: `#mythology #<culture>myth #shorts`
(e.g. `#mythology #norsemythology #shorts`). Nothing else: no links, no email, no keyword lists.

## Tags (backend): 5–8 targeted
Example (Tyr): `norse mythology, tyr and fenrir, fenrir wolf, norse gods, mythology explained, mythology shorts`.

## Altered or synthetic content ("AI disclosure")
Answer **No**: stylised 3D animation made in code, a designed (not cloned) narrator voice, nothing realistic that could
be mistaken for real footage, and no real person's likeness. If a video ever uses a cloned real voice or realistic
footage of real places/people, answer **Yes**. On TikTok, turn on "AI-generated content" (TikTok counts realistic AI
voices).

## Pinned comment
A question that makes people answer: "Would you have pulled the hand out? 🐺" / "Which god would you trust with your life?"

## Thumbnail / cover
Shorts: pick the cover frame in the app (the hook frame: YOU + the impossible thing, big, centred upper half).
Long-form compilations (later): 3 SeaArt prompts per video, the hero huge in the foreground, 2-word text overlay.

## Cross-posting
Same MP4 on TikTok and Instagram Reels; caption = title + 3 hashtags; never post with another app's watermark.

## Checklist file `story/upload.md`
Title · description (2 lines) · tags · AI answer · pinned comment · cover frame time · posting time.

# Bible — "Forgive the Past" (74 s, 9:16)

**Logline.** A man has replayed his last conversation with someone (he was 19) for years, rehearsing the perfect comeback.
The narrator reframes forgiveness: not absolution, but putting down a case you've been prosecuting for a trial that will
never happen — and the twist: you were never the judge, you were the one serving the sentence.
**Tone:** drama, quiet and intimate. "You" and "I" are the same person: HERO.
**Colour arc:** cold blue night → drained grey morning → cold courtroom daylight → warm dawn (only in the last shot).

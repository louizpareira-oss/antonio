# TikTok upload kit — What If You Got 2x Heavier Every Single Day?

Same 16:9 video as YouTube. TikTok captions are searched like keywords, so the first line repeats the title words.
No chapters (TikTok doesn't use them) and no email (it isn't clickable in a caption; it goes in the bio).

## Caption (paste everything between the lines)
Same pattern as YouTube (`docs/12-viral-descriptions.md`): the title question, a "Sounds amazing. Until…" turn and one
specific comment question. 7 hashtags.

## Settings
- Cover: upload the YouTube thumbnail (`thumbnail.jpg`) if the app offers it, otherwise pick the frame where you sink in the lake.
- AI-generated content: on (the voices are AI, and TikTok's rule covers realistic AI audio).
- Comments, Duet, Stitch: on.

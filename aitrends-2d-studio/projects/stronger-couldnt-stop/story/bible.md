# Bible — What If You Got Stronger and Couldn't Stop?

**Logline:** every morning your strength doubles. It's a superpower for a week, a curse by day eight, and on day
thirteen it saves your baby sister, because you finally learned to be gentle. Then the twist: Lily has it too.

**Tone:** comedy (days 1–8), then wonder (famous), tender drama (Lily, the egg test), action (the earthquake), science,
and a cliffhanger. "You" is the same hero as video 1 (blue hoodie), so the channel has a face.

**Brightness rule (CLAUDE.md):** a brighter picture than video 1, with the centre of the frame around 85–100 out of
255.
- Day and interior scenes use `golden`, `day` or `interiorWarm`, with lit walls; little `shade(-0.4)` darkening.
- Night scenes keep bright practical lights (windows, lamps, screens) and big faces.

## Locations (sets)
- **kitchen / living room:** bright morning (`interiorWarm`); big window left; flashback version is a warm night with
  a pendant lamp.
- **bedroom:** morning sun, or blue night.
- **street:** `golden` by day, `night` for the van and the road.
- **classroom, phone shop, hallway, gym, office:** small interiors with one bright window or a light panel.
- **stadium (pitch), harbour, rail, building site:** wide, bright exteriors for the "famous" montage.
- **hospital:** warm room with a big window at night, plus a lit facade.
- **garage:** warm work lamp and an orange glow.
- **Atlas:** the mountain (dusk or dawn, tunnel light), the steel room (cool steel, spotlight on the table, an
  observation glass), and the visit room (glass wall).
- **rubble (earthquake):** night, firefighters' red and white lights, dust in the air.
- **nursery:** soft night light and a moon window.
- **blueprint:** navy grid for the science part.

## Motifs
- **DAY / ×N badge,** top-left: gold for strength, coral when it is dangerous. It updates on the beat.
- **Gold impact rings and spiderweb cracks:** what your strength does to things.
- **The egg:** a symbol of being gentle. The pencil and phone: being too strong.
- **"Strong is easy, gentle is hard":** said by Dad twice, plus the arm-wrestling tally board. "YOU 1" at the end.
- **Glass:** the world is fragile. The visit-room glass wall ends your arc.

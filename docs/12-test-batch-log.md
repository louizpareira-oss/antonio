## Your checklist (before spending on more)

For each clip, in order of importance:

- [ ] **Face and costume stay the same** for the whole 5 seconds: mask, hat, cape, gloves (Sleeve), scarf and basket (Pip).
- [ ] **No text, digits or garbled marks** appear, especially on the tags.
- [ ] **Motion is smooth and readable**, not jittery or warping.
- [ ] **Shot 3:** one eyebrow stays raised. This is his signature.
- [ ] **Shot 5:** the camera really drops into the hat and ends on black. This starts the story, so it matters most.
- [ ] **Style match:** clips look like the keyframes (bold outlines, flat colours).
- [ ] **Sleeve in shot 1 matches Sleeve in shot 3.** If two shots look like different raccoons, the reference did not hold.

If a shot fails, say which one and why. A redo of one keyframe is 15 credits and one 540p clip is 50.

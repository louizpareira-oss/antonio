# Thumbnail — What If You Could Talk to Animals?

Three original concepts for the user to generate in SeaArt (rules in `CLAUDE.md`, Oct 20: 2D cel-shaded, close-up
only, character 50–60 % of the frame). Each prompt starts with the studio's required block, word for word. The user
adds the 2-word text.

**SeaArt negative prompt (all three):**
`3d render, octane, photorealistic, realistic texture, wide shot, tiny character, full street, cross-section, human skin, realistic face, thick body, chibi, Pixar, text, letters, numbers, logo, watermark, signs, clutter, small objects, extra limbs`

## 1. "IT ANSWERED." — a giant whale breaches right behind him (the twist)

```
16:9 aspect ratio, YouTube thumbnail. 2D cel-shaded cartoon illustration style with bold black outlines, flat vibrant colors, and dramatic comic-book glow. A slender stick-figure with thin limbs, a solid black spherical head, and glowing white eyes, wearing a bright blue hoodie. Close-up: the stick-figure is huge in the foreground, taking up 50-60% of the frame, his facial expression big and readable on a phone screen. High contrast, dramatic cyan and neon orange glow. Strictly NO human skin, NO realistic faces, NO thick/chibi bodies, NO 3D render, NO realistic textures.
Close-up on the right side of the frame: he spins toward the camera with one open hand thrust at the lens, big and foreshortened, eyes huge with shock, mouth open. Glowing cyan sound rings ripple from the whale toward his head. Simple flat dawn sky in orange and pink. The left 40% of the frame is clean flat sky, empty space for text. No text, no letters, no numbers, no signs.
```

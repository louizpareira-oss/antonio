WHAT IF YOU HAD AN INVENTORY IN REAL LIFE? — IMAGE PROMPT PACK ("Toy Box" style)
45 scenes. Numbers match S01-S45 in scripts/01-inventory-in-real-life.md.
The full style rules are in what-if/02-style-guide.md.

THE LOOK
Soft vinyl-toy characters in a miniature diorama world. Sprout is the only cream-colored
character; everyone else is a pastel bean (mint, peach, lilac, sky blue). There are no outlines.
Daylight scenes use a sherbet palette. As things go wrong the colors cool toward teal, and the
ending is set at night in deep teal.

HOW TO USE
- Each prompt below is complete. Copy one line and paste it into your image generator.
- "--ar 16:9" and "--no" are Midjourney syntax. In other generators, delete them, pick 16:9
  in the settings, and put "outlines, stick figure, neck, purple" in the negative prompt.
- Do this first: generate Sprout alone on a plain sky-blue background (prompt at the bottom
  of this file). Pick the best one, save it as sprout-reference.png, and use it as the
  character reference for all 45 scenes.
- The inventory grid, slot icons, counters and on-screen words are NOT in these prompts,
  because image generators garble small text and grids. Add them in your editor as sticker
  graphics; each "EDIT:" line says what goes on top of that shot.
- For motion, run each image through image-to-video for a 3-5 second clip.
- Palette by section: S01-S27 day, S28-S38 turning (S29 is a night shot), S39-S45 night.

CHARACTER (already included in every prompt):
cute 3D vinyl-toy character: a soft cream-colored bean-shaped body with no neck (head and body are one rounded capsule), short stubby arms and legs, big glossy black oval eyes with white highlights and a small simple mouth printed on the front, a small two-leaf green sprout growing from the top, wearing an oversized mustard-yellow hoodie with long sleeves

STYLE (already included in every prompt):
miniature diorama world, soft matte vinyl and clay toy materials, rounded chunky shapes, tilt-shift depth of field, soft diffused light with gentle contact shadows, high quality 3D render, no text --ar 16:9 --no outlines, stick figure, neck, purple
